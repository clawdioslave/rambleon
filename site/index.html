Rambleon

Your Azeroth adventure journal

Play World of Warcraft normally. Rambleon quietly remembers the night. When you log out, it hands you a permanent record: a timeline, a factual log, a story page, and a journal chapter written from what actually happened, readable in game and ready to paste anywhere.

It is a memory layer, not a meter: it never automates anything, never reads protected combat data, and keeps your history on your own Mac.
