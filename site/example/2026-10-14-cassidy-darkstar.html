Chapter 17 — A Meeting in the Cathedral of Light

Cassidy Darkstar · October 14, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar, Night Elf and Druid, took up his road again in Stormwind City, and the first place his feet found was the Cathedral of Light. He came there in the full measure of his strength as it then stood, at the six-and-twentieth mark of his long apprenticeship, and he went no higher in this chapter. That was no shame. Not every stage of a long road is a climb, and some are only a standing-still between one going and the next.

It was in the Cathedral of Light that Cassidy met a fellow traveller, and joined forces with him. This was Rambleon, a Hunter, and from that meeting they were companions on the road. Of what words passed between them the record keeps nothing, and nothing shall be added here. It is enough that two who had walked apart now walked together, a Druid and a Hunter, and that the Night Elf did not go on alone. Such meetings are small things in the telling. Yet the greater tales are often made from them, and many a long fellowship began with no more than a joining of hands under one roof.

No errand was taken up and none laid down. No foe was met, no spoil was gathered, and Cassidy came to no new country beyond the hall where he had begun. He did not fall. The chapter was a quiet one, and it closed with no formal leave-taking, the way a traveller sometimes rests by the wayside without marking the place, meaning to rise and go on. Cassidy Darkstar of the Night Elves, in the city of Men, with Rambleon the Hunter at his side, might well have been glad of the company, for a road is lighter when it is shared. The tale had gathered one more name into it, and that is not nothing.

Whatever roads lay beyond the doors of the Cathedral of Light, they waited still, and the two companions had not yet set foot upon them.
