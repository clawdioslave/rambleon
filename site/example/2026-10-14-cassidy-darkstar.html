Chapter 17 — The Crossing to Southshore

Cassidy Darkstar · October 14, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar's road began beneath the roof of the Cathedral of Light in Stormwind City. There he joined with Rambleon, a hunter, and he joined with him a second time soon after, as if the first bond had not quite held and had to be made again. From then on the two were companions, and Rambleon kept beside him for the whole of the way that followed. Together they went out into Cathedral Square, and from the square the tale moves on to other lands.

Next the record names the Deepwater Tavern, in the Lion's Pride Inn, and Cassidy kept a picture of his coming there. Then Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands, where he took another. The harbor did not let him go at once. He went out to Baradin Bay and came back to Menethil Harbor, then turned again to Baradin Bay. Afterwards he passed into The Great Sea, and the waters of Baradin Bay and The Great Sea came one after the other until the Wetlands lay behind him. He came into the reaches of the Great Sea that belong to the Hillsbrad Foothills, and he kept a third picture at that crossing.

He came ashore on the Eastern Strand, passed on to the Western Strand, and so entered Southshore. There his errands found him. He took up "A Lack of Virtue," which charged him to carry Tom's Gift to Bart Tidewater in Menethil Harbor, back across the water he had just crossed. Soon afterward he finished "The Shamed Lieutenant," the one task of that road he saw through to its end, and he grew somewhat in experience for it. The people of Southshore gave him more work. He accepted "Down the Coast," and after it "Brewer's Trade." Then he went into Southshore Town Hall, kept a last picture as he entered, and there took up "Syndicate Assassins," an errand whose name alone gave warning of what it would ask of him. He came out again into Southshore with more charges laid on him than he had brought into the town.

No blade was drawn and no foe fell on this stretch of the road. It was a journey of passage, from the halls of Stormwind over the harbors and bays of the Wetlands to a new shore, made in good company. Cassidy did not grow in strength on this journey, but he had come a long way and gathered many errands. Some of those errands pointed back toward Menethil Harbor, and others pointed down the coast and out into the hills, and every one of them waited on his choosing.
