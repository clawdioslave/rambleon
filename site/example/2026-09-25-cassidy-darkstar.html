Chapter 2 — The Crown of the Earth

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out again from Dolanaar and went down among the Gnarlpine, where shaman and defender, warrior and pathfinder fell before him one after another. He cleansed three fel cones for the errand called Seek Redemption!, and in the woods he came into the eighth measure of his strength. At Gnarlpine Hold he found the glowing fruit and carried it to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. In Darnassus he completed Eyes of the Sentinels within the Temple of the Moon, and he gathered six soft pelts from the nightsabers of the wild. At the lake he took the fifteenth Gnarlpine fang for the bounty and grew into his ninth level.

He went on into ground he had not walked before: the Pools of Arlithrien, Ban'ethil Hollow and its Barrow Den, and at last The Oracle Glade. There he finished The Lost Runner and took up Not Dead Yet and The Enchanted Glade, and he filled an amethyst phial for the old errand of the Crown of the Earth. The Bloodfeather harpies gave up six belts to him. Once, in those high places, the road threw him down for a while, and he rose and went on. At Dolanaar he saw Not Dead Yet through, and then, among the lashers of Lake Al'Ameth, the errand of The Great Tree Provides, and he took up the salve meant for Sentinel Eralya Leafshadow.

The road grew less lonely at Fel Rock. With Cayde the hunter and Koalla the druid beside him for a short way, he struck down Lord Melenas and took his head, and back at Dolanaar he came into his tenth level. Doom the hunter went with him through The Cleft, and Marta the warrior walked with them briefly, until Xethorr the Wicked fell and the fel moss was won. Afterward Cassidy returned to Shadowglen and Aldrassil where his road had begun, and there he finished Nature's Call and The Goddess Provides. Beyond Starbreeze Village, Eo, Harcos, Thunder and Iwill stood with him a little while against Ferocitas the Dream Eater, and Tallonkai's Jewel was recovered. At the lake Subrin and Lunaria shared the labour for longer, and Edrelyse the druid kept company with him for the whole walk into Darnassus.

In the Cenarion Enclave Cassidy heeded the call of his order and was sent onward toward Moonglade, and there he came into his eleventh level. He then carried the Crown of the Earth to its end, passing it from Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm to Lariia and finally to Tyrande Whisperwind in the Temple of the Moon. When he came back to Denalan he was sent against Oakenscowl, and with Kaner and Volpixi at his side the gargantuan tumor was taken. In the Barrow Den, with Mil the priest as his faithful companion, he gathered the relics of wakening one by one, the rune, the quill, the sapphire and the talisman, and heard of the Sleeping Druid and of Ursal the Mauler.

Last of all Cassidy climbed again to The Oracle Glade and delivered the salve he had carried so long to its sentinel. With Not the warrior beside him he overcame Hatescreech and Windmistress Gaedress and took both amulets for Darkness in the Glade. He accepted a new errand there, to bring a Shimmering Frond to Denalan, and the road led down once more toward the lake.
