Chapter 2 — The Glade and the Bounty

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026 · 2h 33m in Azeroth

Cassidy Darkstar set out again from Dolanaar in the late morning and went into the wide woods of Teldrassil. There he fought the Gnarlpine, their shamans and defenders and warriors, and the Nightsaber Stalker and the Webwood Venomfang. Before long he had gathered the three Fel Cones that the errand called "Seek Redemption!" required. In that same hour he came into greater strength and reached his eighth level. A picture was taken to mark it.

From there he went on to Gnarlpine Hold, where a glowing fruit came into his keeping, to be carried to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. He fought the Avengers and Pathfinders of the Hold. The road then turned him toward Darnassus, and he passed through Tradesmen's Terrace and The Temple Gardens to the Temple of the Moon. There he laid down the errand of "Eyes of the Sentinels." Back in Dolanaar he finished "Seek Redemption!" and then "Soft Saber Pelts," for he had taken six soft pelts from the nightsabers of the forest. He went up to Fel Rock and fought the Shadow Sprites and Rascal Sprites there. Once he fell, and the road went on. At Lake Al'Ameth he gave the glowing fruit to Denalan as he had been asked.

The Gnarlpine did not escape his reckoning. Near Lake Al'Ameth he gathered the last of fifteen Gnarlpine Fangs, and in that same hour he reached his ninth level. He went west past the Pools of Arlithrien and came to Ban'ethil Hollow and the Barrow Den beneath it. Afterwards the bounty on the Gnarlpine furbolgs was claimed. Then he went north to The Oracle Glade. There he saw "The Lost Runner" to its end and took up two new errands, "Not Dead Yet" and "The Enchanted Glade." He also filled the Amethyst Phial that the task "Crown of the Earth" asked of him. The Bloodfeather harpies of that place, sorceresses and rogues alike, stood against him. He took six of their belts and was given a darker charge, "Darkness in the Glade." Along the Wellspring River he met the Timberling Mire Beast and the Elder Timberling for the first time.

In the early afternoon he came back to Dolanaar. There "Not Dead Yet" was finished, and he drew Refreshing Spring Water for "The Great Tree Provides." The first part of "Crown of the Earth" was completed, and its next step was given to him. The Filled Vessel was now to be brought to Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm in Darnassus. Yet he did not go at once. He went down again to Lake Al'Ameth, and the last things set down that day were a Lasher Sproutling and a Timberling slain on its shores. The vessel was still in his keeping, and the way to Darnassus lay before him.
