Chapter 2 — The Crown of the Earth

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out again from Dolanaar into the wood of Teldrassil, and the Gnarlpine were the first to feel his coming: shaman and defender, warrior and augur, until the fel cones were gathered and the errand called Seek Redemption was answered. In Gnarlpine Hold he found the glowing fruit and bore it to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. In the Temple of the Moon he closed the matter of the Eyes of the Sentinels. The soft pelts of the nightsabers were brought in, and the bounty on the Gnarlpine furbolg was paid in fangs. It was not all easy going, but he did not turn aside.

Northward lay The Oracle Glade, where The Lost Runner was found at last, and the Bloodfeather harpies held the air. Cassidy went up among them more than once, and in the end Hatescreech, Windmistress Gaedress and Witchmother Arysa were overthrown, and the Darkness in the Glade was lifted. The Oracle Tree gave him Inscribed Bark for Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm. Meanwhile the long errand of the Crown of the Earth drew him to and fro: a filled vessel to Fandral in the Cenarion Enclave, a drained one to Lariia, and the Moonwell Remnants to Tyrande Whisperwind herself in the Temple of the Moon. He did lesser kindnesses as well. The Great Tree Provides was seen through, and the Lashwater Salve went to Sentinel Eralya Leafshadow on the road to the Oracle Tree.

He did not always walk alone. At Fel Rock, with Cayde the hunter and Koalla the druid beside him, Lord Melenas fell. In The Cleft, Doom and Marta stood with him when Xethorr the Wicked was slain, and so Twisted Hatred was ended. Harcos, Thunder and Iwill were there when Ferocitas the Dream Eater gave up Tallonkai's Jewel. Subrin and Lunaria helped him against the timberlings of the lake, and Kaner and Volpixi were near when the great tumour of Oakenscowl was taken. In Ban'ethil Barrow Den he sought the Relics of Wakening and woke The Sleeping Druid, and he learned what it meant to be a Druid of the Claw. The Barrow Den was hard ground, and not every venture there went his way. Mil the priest kept company with him longest of all. Justín and Victoza followed near as far, and Tina, Elundra and Shivv each walked some part of that road. Many others passed by with a nod and were gone, and all were courteous. Somewhere between the Glade and the Den, Cassidy came into his strength, rising from the seventh rank to the twelfth, and a picture was taken when the last of it came upon him.

Ursal the Mauler was slain in Gnarlpine Hold with Elora at his side. Sathrah's Sacrifice was made in the Temple of the Moon, and the Sprouted Fronds were given into Denalan's keeping. Heeding the Call, Cassidy had been named for Moonglade. Fandral had set a message in his hands for Onu in the Grove of the Ancients in Darkshore, and Nessa Shadowsong awaited him too. Every glade and hollow of Teldrassil had now been walked. So he came down at last to Rut'theran Village at the tree's foot, where the roots meet the sea, and beyond it the road ran on toward Darkshore.
