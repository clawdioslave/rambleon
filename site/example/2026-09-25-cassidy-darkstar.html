Chapter 2 — The Fangs of the Gnarlpine

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026 · 1h 45m in Azeroth

Cassidy Darkstar set out again from Dolanaar in the late morning, and the road did not go easily with him at first. Scarcely had he passed out into the wider woods of Teldrassil when he fell. The fall came so early that it seemed almost a part of the setting out, and a picture was taken of that moment. He woke again in Dolanaar and went back into the forest, and this time he held his ground. The Gnarlpine were the first he met: shaman and defender, and later a warrior of their kin. After them came a Nightsaber Stalker and a Webwood Venomfang. At the end of that hard stretch he gathered the third of the Fel Cones he had been sent for, and in that same hour he came into the strength of his eighth level.

From there his path led into Gnarlpine Hold. There he took up a new errand, called The Glowing Fruit, which was to bring the fruit to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. He went twice into the Hold. He slew an Avenger of the furbolgs there and a Pathfinder, and in the Hold he fell a second time. His return to life brought him to Darnassus. He passed along the Tradesmen's Terrace and through the Temple Gardens and so came to the Temple of the Moon, where he laid down the errand named Eyes of the Sentinels. Then he turned back to Dolanaar and saw the matter of Seek Redemption through to its end.

The day after that was spent in many short labours. Nightsabers and a Timberling fell before him, and when he had six Soft Nightsaber Pelts he brought them to Dolanaar and was done with that task. He went up to Fel Rock and fought the Shadow Sprites and Rascal Sprites of that place, and there he fell for the third time. He rose again at Dolanaar and walked down to Lake Al'Ameth, where he brought the glowing fruit to Denalan as he had been asked. On the lake's shores he slew a Gnarlpine Augur, and there he fell once more. Yet he did not turn aside. He came back to the water and fought on, and he slew a Webwood Lurker among others, until at last he held fifteen Gnarlpine Fangs for the bounty on the furbolgs. In that same hour he came into his ninth level, and a picture was taken to mark it.

Then he went west, by the Pools of Arlithrien, to Ban'ethil Hollow and down into the Ban'ethil Barrow Den. There he fell for the fifth time that day. He was raised in Darnassus again and went back out into Teldrassil, and there he claimed the Bounty on the Gnarlpine Furbolg. He took one more picture at that moment of reward, a quiet close to a long labour. Last of all Cassidy walked north and came to the Oracle Glade. He stood at its edge for a while with the forest behind him, and whatever waited beyond it he had not yet seen.
