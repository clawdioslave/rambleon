Chapter 2 — Five Falls Under Teldrassil

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026 · 1h 45m in Azeroth

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Dolanaar in the late morning, and the road did not favour him at first. He had gone only a little way into the wider woods of Teldrassil when he fell, and he woke among the living again at Dolanaar. He took it without complaint and went back out. In the next hour he fought his way through the Gnarlpine: shamans, defenders and warriors, and after them a Nightsaber Stalker and a Webwood Venomfang. As the third Fel Cone was gathered for the errand called "Seek Redemption!", he came into the strength of his eighth level, and a picture was taken to mark it.

From there he went on to Gnarlpine Hold, and there he took up a new charge, "The Glowing Fruit": the fruit was to be brought to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. He met the Avenger and the Pathfinder of the furbolgs in that place, and there he fell a second time. He returned to life in Darnassus, and he did not hurry back to the fighting. He passed through the Tradesmen's Terrace and The Temple Gardens and came to the Temple of the Moon, where he completed "Eyes of the Sentinels". Then he took the long way down again to Dolanaar and completed "Seek Redemption!" there.

After that the day was busy, as days in the forest often are. Around Lake Al'Ameth he hunted nightsabers and timberlings until six Soft Nightsaber Pelts were gathered, and back in Dolanaar he completed "Soft Saber Pelts". At Fel Rock he fought Shadow Sprites and Rascal Sprites, and it was there that he fell a third time. He got up at Dolanaar, went to Lake Al'Ameth, and completed "The Glowing Fruit", so the errand he had taken up at Gnarlpine Hold was finished at last. Near the lake a Gnarlpine Augur fell to him, and soon after he fell himself, the fourth time that day, and he woke again at Dolanaar. He went back to the lake and slew a Webwood Lurker. Near the water the fifteenth Gnarlpine Fang was counted for "Bounty: Gnarlpine Furbolg", and at that same hour he came into his ninth level. That moment too was kept in a picture.

The last stretch led him westward and north, past the Pools of Arlithrien to Ban'ethil Hollow, and down into the Ban'ethil Barrow Den. There he fell for the fifth time. He woke in Darnassus, came back into Teldrassil, and completed "Bounty: Gnarlpine Furbolg", and a picture was taken of that hour as well. In all, fifty-five foes had fallen to him since morning, and he had walked a good many paths of his homeland that were new to him. Last of all he came to The Oracle Glade, and there, with the road still going on before him, Cassidy Darkstar set down his journey for the day.
