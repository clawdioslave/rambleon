Chapter 2 — The Five Falls of Teldrassil

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026 · 1h 55m in Azeroth

In the late morning Cassidy Darkstar set out again from Dolanaar, and he had gone only a little way into the wider woods of Teldrassil before he fell, within the first hour's quarter. It was a poor beginning. A picture was taken at that moment, and soon he stood among the living in Dolanaar once more and went back out the way he had come. After that the road went better. Among the Gnarlpine he slew their shamans, their defenders and their warriors, and he brought down a Nightsaber Stalker and a Webwood Venomfang. When he had gathered three Fel Cones, he came into his eighth level of strength, and he marked the hour with a picture.

From there he went to Gnarlpine Hold, and there he took up the errand of the glowing fruit, which was to be carried to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth. He turned back into the Hold, where he met a Gnarlpine Avenger and a Pathfinder, and in that place he fell a second time. He came back to life in Darnassus. He walked the Tradesmen's Terrace and The Temple Gardens and came at last to the Temple of the Moon, where he finished the errand called "Eyes of the Sentinels." Then he went down again to Dolanaar and handed over the Fel Cones he had gathered. He hunted the Nightsaber and the Timberling near Lake Al'Ameth until six soft pelts were counted, and he was paid for them in Dolanaar.

Fel Rock came next. There he fought Shadow Sprites and Rascal Sprites, and there he fell a third time. He took no long rest after it. He went straight to Lake Al'Ameth and gave the glowing fruit to Denalan, and so that errand was ended. He fell a fourth time on the lake's southern edge, after a Gnarlpine Augur had been slain. He went back to the water, and when the fifteenth Gnarlpine Fang was in his keeping, he came into his ninth level. He noted it with a picture and went on westward.

He passed the Pools of Arlithrien and came to Ban'ethil Hollow, and he went down into the Ban'ethil Barrow Den. He fell there for the fifth time that day and came back once more in Darnassus. He did not linger. Soon the bounty on the Gnarlpine furbolg was paid, and a picture was taken. His way then led north to The Oracle Glade. There he finished "The Lost Runner" and took up two new errands, "Not Dead Yet" and "The Enchanted Glade." He filled the Amethyst Phial for "Crown of the Earth" and fought the Bloodfeather harpies, their sorceresses and their rogues. The day's account stops there, with Cassidy still in the glade and the road north still before him.
