Chapter 4 — The Stand at Windfield Orchard

Axilla Interstellar · October 21, 2026

Axilla Interstellar set out from Shen'dar Village with the errand called Taming the Beast, and went down to Windsong Lake. There she fell in with Winterland, a hunter as she was, and the two kept company to the end of that road and did not part. From Windsong Lake Axilla drew up the Enchanted Gyrozephyr for the matter of the Broken Construct. In Shen'dar Highlands she fell once, and rose again, and went on. In Valanaar she laid down the old tasks one after another: Taming the Beast, Unwelcome Spirits, The Fate of the Den, Aid For The Refugees, and The Fate of a Loved One, the last of which had a grave name.

Then the companions turned to the Gustberry Lowlands, where Bandit Highwaymen kept the roads, and many of them were overthrown. From the Bandit Camp Axilla won the Air Construct Core, and more than once in those lowlands she paused to take a picture, as one marks a place worth remembering. Beyond lay Windfield Orchard, and there she found Aamelia Windfield, and the errand of the Blood Tithe was ended. Aamelia had other work: she bid Axilla to make herself useful, to thin the Ornery Galestriders, and to gather Flutterfly Dust. Hungry Bandits fell, ripe Stormapples were gathered, and with that labour Axilla came into new strength, the thirteenth mark of her growing.

Much going to and fro followed between the orchard and Valanaar. The Broken Construct was set right at last; A Firm Response was taken up; the road passed by Fairweather Stables. When the Galestriders had yielded their tenderloins and the Flutterflies their dust, Aamelia sent her against the bandits once more, and seven blood-stained masks were brought back. Then came the errand called Standing Our Ground. Axilla followed Aamelia and made a final stand beside her, and it held, and she bore the news of it to Valanaar. After that she went out and confronted Belathaan Brightwish, a Vuldren Alpha falling on the way, and so the Firm Response was given.

From Valanaar she was sent in the service of Zephras to the East Pylon Watchtower, and the Al'Aketh were found before it. Healers and brawlers of that cult were cut down until their strength was broken, and Commander Belguilos himself was slain. In the doing of it Axilla grew again, to the fourteenth mark. She took ten Windstone Charms from the Al'Aketh in answer to Blood for Blood, and returned to Valanaar at the High Elder's request. There a new matter was laid on her: a turncoat. Past Al'Aketh Assassins she went into Shen'dar Highlands, found the secluded house, and found the Turncoat within, and she carried a glowing crystal back to Talanaanis Shadowsong. What could be learned from it was learned, and the Cult's true plans came to light. Times were desperate now, and Axilla went to speak with Valennia Stormfist. With Winterland still at her side, she stood ready for whatever word Valennia would give.
