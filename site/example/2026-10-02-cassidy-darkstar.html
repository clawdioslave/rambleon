Chapter 9 — The Long Road to Westfall

Cassidy Darkstar · October 2, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Lake Elune'ara in the Moonglade, and he did not go alone, for at the very start of the road he joined company with Rambleon, a Hunter. They kept together from first step to last, and no word of their parting is set down. From the Moonglade the two came to Darnassus. There Cassidy passed back and forth between the Craftsmen's Terrace and the city's paths, as one does when leaving a place long known, and as he entered Darnassus he took a picture to keep. Then they went down to Rut'theran Village in Teldrassil, and another picture was taken at the threshold of that land.

After that they crossed The Veiled Sea. They came first to Darkshore, where Cassidy marked the moment with a picture, and then to Mist's Edge and Auberdine. They lingered only briefly along The Long Wash before they turned to the water again. Once more The Veiled Sea lay behind them, and they came into Stormwind Harbor. As he set foot in Stormwind City, Cassidy took a picture once more. From the harbour the companions went by The Canals and through The Park into the Mage Quarter. There Cassidy saw through the errand called "Devils in Westfall" and laid it down as finished. In the same place he took up a new charge, "Special Delivery for Gaxim": he was to bring the Scroll of Messaging to Gaxim Rustfizzle in Stonetalon. That road runs far from where his feet were bent that night, and he carried the scroll with him all the same.

Then they went on by The Canals again and through the Trade District, out by the Valley of Heroes and into Elwynn Forest. They passed Goldshire and Forest's Edge, and so came into Westfall, and Cassidy took a picture there as well. Their road led by The Jansen Stead, Furlbrow's Pumpkin Farm and Saldean's Farm, and twice it brought them to The Molsen Farm. Somewhere among those fields Cassidy gathered eight handfuls of oats for the errand called "Poor Old Blanchy", and that part of the task was done.

At the Alexston Farmstead he faced a Harvest Watcher, the first of its kind to fall to him, and he overcame it. He took a picture there, and he also took up the errand named "... and that note you found". Then the companions went on to Moonbrook and from there into The Dagger Hills. In those hills he accepted a further charge, called "Thunderbrew". It was there too that a Defias Pathstalker fell before him, the first of that brotherhood to do so, and he took one last picture to remember it by. Cassidy went down to Longshore and climbed back into The Dagger Hills, and he gained no new level that night.

He had a great many lands behind him now, from the Moonglade to the hills of Westfall, and a scroll for Stonetalon was still in his pack. With Rambleon still beside him, Cassidy turned his face to the road ahead.
