Chapter 9 — The Road to Ironclad Cove

Cassidy Darkstar · October 2, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Lake Elune'ara in Moonglade, and he had hardly begun before Rambleon the Hunter joined him. Rambleon stayed with him for the whole of that long road, from its first step to its last. Together they passed through the Craftsmen's Terrace and Darnassus, and so down to Rut'theran Village. From there they crossed the Veiled Sea to Darkshore, by way of Mist's Edge, Auberdine and the Long Wash. Then the sea carried them again, and they came at last to Stormwind Harbor. At each new border Cassidy paused to take a picture.

In the Mage Quarter of Stormwind City he brought the errand called Devils in Westfall to its end. There he took up another: a Scroll of Messaging, to be carried to Gaxim Rustfizzle in Stonetalon. That road would have to wait, for his way now ran west. He went through the Trade District and the Valley of Heroes, into Elwynn Forest, past Goldshire and out by the Forest's Edge into Westfall. He crossed the Jansen Stead, Furlbrow's Pumpkin Farm, Saldean's Farm and the Molsen Farm, and on the way he gathered eight handfuls of oats for Poor Old Blanchy. At the Alexston Farmstead he slew his first Harvest Watcher, and he took up the errand of a note he had found there. He then went south through Moonbrook to the Dagger Hills. There he accepted Thunderbrew and later Collecting Memories, and he drove off Defias Pathstalkers, Knuckledusters and Highwaymen near Longshore and Demont's Place.

Then he went down into the Deadmines. Cortonna the Mage stood with him only briefly at the door of the Defias Hideout. Dire the Warrior came soon after and stayed for the long descent, and so did Jstermy the Hunter and Emchetyre the Mage, both of whom joined him in the depths. With Rambleon they made five, and they cut their way through Defias Miners, Diggers and Strip Miners beyond easy counting. One after another they overcame the old names of that place. Marisa du'Paige fell, and then Rhahk'Zor. In the Mast Room they cast down Sneed and his Shredder, and with that Gnoam Sprecklesprocket was answered for the errand of the Underground Assault. In the Goblin Foundry, among Craftsmen and Engineers, they brought down Gilnid. There was a fall in those depths, as there often is on such roads, but the company did not turn aside. They pressed on past Defias Pirates and Squallshapers into Ironclad Cove, and there they slew Blackguards. Many pictures were taken along the way.

At Sentinel Hill the company broke up. Jstermy, Dire and Emchetyre went their own ways with Cassidy's thanks, but Rambleon stayed at his side. The two of them walked out to the Dead Acre, where Harvest Reapers fell before them, along with a Greater Fleshripper. Then they came back to Sentinel Hill and lingered at its edges. Cassidy was the same in strength as when he set out, but much richer in deeds. The scroll for Gaxim was still in his pack, and the roads of Westfall still lay open before him.
