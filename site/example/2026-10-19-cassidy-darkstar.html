Chapter 20 — The Charred Vale and the Road to Ratchet

Cassidy Darkstar · October 19, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Tower of Althalaxx in Darkshore, and there for a brief while he stood beside Nerved, a paladin, and Sethra, a warlock, though their fellowship was soon ended and each went his own way. Then he took the long road south, by Mist's Edge and Auberdine, by The Long Wash and Twilight Shore. He crossed The Zoram Strand into Ashenvale, and he paused there to take a picture. He passed the edge of The Veiled Sea into the Stonetalon Mountains and took another. By Stonetalon Peak he came down at last to Mirkfallon Lake, where the first of the Blackened Basilisks fell before him.

The Charred Vale was the heart of his labour. There the Bloodfury held the ground, and Cassidy went against them in the errand called Reclaiming the Charred Vale. Ambusher, Slayer, Storm Witch and Roguefeather fell to him, until he had slain six apiece of the Ambushers, the Slayers and the Roguefeathers as the task required. Among them fell Scorched, Blackened and Singed Basilisks, a Blackened Ancient and a Fledgling Chimaera. Once he fell there himself, but he rose and returned to the Vale and did not leave it until the work was done. He gathered the eight Crystalized Scales for Mauren, and for a short stretch Scoob, a warrior, fought at his side, and together they brought down a Furious Stone Spirit. Many times in that valley Cassidy stopped to take a picture, as though he wished to keep the place in memory.

From the Vale he went by Mirkfallon Lake and the Webwinder Path. There he saw the Special Delivery for Gaxim through to its end and took up Castpipe's Task. At Windshear Crag, hard by Blackwolf River, he recovered the Super Reaper 6000 Blueprints and finished that errand. In return he received Further Instructions and a sealed envelope meant for Sputtervalve in the Barrens. So he passed through Greatwood Vale and Camp Aparaje and came into The Barrens, where he took a picture. From Honor's Stand Coked, a warrior, walked with him a little way, past The Forgotten Pools and the Shrine of the Fallen Warrior to the Gold Road, and there they parted.

At Ratchet the envelope found its end, and new instructions were given. There Cassidy took up many errands: Blueleaf Tubers, for which he received a Crate With Holes, a Snufflenose Owner's Manual and a Snufflenose Command Stick; Deepmoss Spider Eggs; and Raptor Horns. He ranged by The Stagnant Oasis, Southern Barrens and Bramblescar, and he gathered five Intact Raptor Horns. Then he went back to Ratchet, saw that errand done, and took up Smart Drinks in its place. Last of all he went by Lushwater Oasis to the Wailing Caverns, taking a picture at the threshold, and passed down into the Cavern of Mists. There Hilde, a hunter, joined him. She was the longest companion of his journey, and with her he took up Deviate Hides and gathered six Wailing Essences. When Cassidy laid down his road for a while, he stood once more in the Cavern of Mists, with his tasks unfinished and the way still open before him.
