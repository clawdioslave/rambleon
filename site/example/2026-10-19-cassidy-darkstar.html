Chapter 20 — The Charred Vale and the Road to Ratchet

Cassidy Darkstar · October 19, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Tower of Althalaxx in Darkshore, and there for a little while he had company. Nerved, a paladin, and Sethra, a warlock, joined with him and went their ways again almost before the road had begun, Sethra first and Nerved soon after. Then Cassidy went on alone, by Mist's Edge and Auberdine, by The Long Wash and Twilight Shore, until he came down onto The Zoram Strand in Ashenvale and passed over The Veiled Sea. A picture was taken as he crossed into Ashenvale, and another as he entered the Stonetalon Mountains, for he marked the leaving of one country and the coming into another.

By Stonetalon Peak and Mirkfallon Lake he went, and there the first Blackened Basilisk fell before him. Then he came to The Charred Vale, and in that place lay the heart of his labour. The errand was named Reclaiming the Charred Vale, and it asked much of him: Bloodfury Ambushers, Bloodfury Slayers and Bloodfury Roguefeathers, six of each, and he saw every part of it through. Storm Witches of the Bloodfury fell as well, and Scorched and Singed Basilisks, a Blackened Ancient, a Fledgling Chimaera, and at the last a Furious Stone Spirit. Once the Vale had the better of him, and he came back by Webwinder Path and Sun Rock Retreat to take up the work where he had left it. He gathered the eight Crystalized Scales for Mauren besides, and many pictures were taken in the Vale as the work went on. Near the end Scoob, a warrior, fought beside him for a short stretch, and they parted when the last Roguefeather had fallen.

Then Cassidy went down the Webwinder Path. There the Special Delivery for Gaxim was finished, and Castpipe's Task was taken up in its place. At Windshear Crag, hard by Blackwolf River, he found the blueprints of the Super Reaper 6000 and saw that errand ended too. In return he was given Further Instructions: a Sealed Envelope, to be carried to Sputtervalve in the Barrens. So he went by Greatwood Vale and Camp Aparaje and came out into The Barrens, and a picture marked that crossing. From Honor's Stand, Coked, a warrior, walked with him a little way, past The Forgotten Pools and the Shrine of the Fallen Warrior and onto the Gold Road, and then they too said farewell.

In Ratchet the envelope was delivered and Further Instructions answered, though only for more instructions to follow. There also he took up three new errands, Blueleaf Tubers, Deepmoss Spider Eggs and Raptor Horns. For the first of these he was given a Crate With Holes, a Snufflenose Owner's Manual and a Snufflenose Command Stick, strange gear for a druid of the forests. Then he turned south past The Stagnant Oasis into the Southern Barrens, and came at last to Bramblescar, and the road ran on before him.
