Chapter 12 — The Banners of Angerfang

Cassidy Darkstar · October 6, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor, and there Rambleon the hunter joined him. Of all who walked with him on that road, Rambleon stayed the longest, and he was still beside Cassidy when they came at last to Darnassus. Together they went by Menethil Bay and Baradin Bay into Bluegill Marsh. There the Bluegill murlocs, puddlejumpers and warriors fell before them, and Cassidy stopped more than once to take a picture. From there the road ran past The Lost Fleet and through Sundown Marsh and Saltspray Glen. The marsh claimed him once, as wild places will. He rose again and went on, and in Sundown Marsh he slew his first crocolisks of the Wetlands.

Beyond lay Angerfang Encampment, and there the heaviest work of the road was done. Natcho, a hunter, joined them on the way and fought at their side through the long struggle against the Dragonmaw. Dazzs the rogue stood with them for a shorter while. Raiders fell by the score, and bonewarders, swamprunners, centurions and shadowwarders with them, and the skeletons that served them. Eight war banners of the Dragonmaw were taken. One by one their attack plans were destroyed: the plan against Menethil Harbor, then Southshore, then Thelsamar. Last of all came the plan against Ironforge itself, with Paladonald the paladin and Quad the mage briefly beside him. Many pictures were taken in that camp.

He went back to Menethil Keep with his errands done, and there "War Banners" and "From the Ashes" were counted fulfilled. A new charge was laid on him, "Nek'rosh's Gambit", to destroy the Dragonmaw catapults. Earlier, on the road outside Angerfang, he had taken up "Razormaw Needling" and "Trying Times". In the Deepwater Tavern he got a flagon of Dwarven honeymead and so finished "The Third Fleet", and in its place he took "The Cursed Crew". Then he went east again to The Green Belt. There "The Greenwarden" was completed and "Tramping Paws" begun. Jaxsul the shaman and Kalos, a druid of his own calling, kept company with him for a while. Among them they put down red whelps and lost whelps, crimson and flamesnorting whelps, and maddened rotclaws, until eight pristine crimson scales had been gathered as far as Direforge Hill. Cassidy brought these back to Menethil Harbor, and "Crocs of the Sky" was done.

After that his road turned toward his own country. He came to Nighthaven in Moonglade and passed by Lake Elune'ara into Darkshore, crossing by the Ruins of Mathystra and Mist's Edge. He went over The Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and back again to Auberdine. At Cliffspring Falls he filled a sampler, and so "The Principal Source" was seen through. He then took up "Gathering the Cure" and gathered twelve lunar fungus for it. After that he crossed once more to Teldrassil and walked in Darnassus, on Tradesmen's Terrace and Warrior's Terrace and in The Temple Gardens. There he parted from Rambleon, his companion of the whole long road. Cassidy went alone through Darkshore as far as Ameth'Aran, and then he stood again in the Deepwater Tavern where he had started, and he took one last picture there. The cure was gathered, the catapults were still standing, and the road lay open before him.
