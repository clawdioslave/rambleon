Chapter 11 — The Relics of the Great Cat

Cassidy Darkstar · October 5, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Darnassus, and his errand took him to Nighthaven in Moonglade, where a druid of his kind goes to learn the deeper secrets of the wild. He took a picture as he entered that country, as a traveller does who knows a place will matter to him. There he took up the "Trial of the Lake". It led him down to the waters of Lake Elune'ara and then on to the Shrine of Remulos, and at the shrine he saw the trial through. Another task was laid on him at once, the "Trial of the Sea Lion". He took it up and carried it with him, and it was still unfinished when the chapter closed.

After this he went down into the Stormrage Barrow Dens. In those halls he found three things he had been sent for, one after another: the Relic of the Silent Shadow, the Relic of the Fang, and the Relic of the Claw. With all three in hand he came up again by the lake, and the errand called "The Great Cat Spirit" was fulfilled. Then he was given the "Blessings of the Great Cat Spirit". He took that charge back across the shores of Lake Elune'ara to Nighthaven and saw it done. Of all the deeds of that night, this was the heart, for a druid does not lightly learn the ways of the cat.

The last errand given him in Moonglade was plain: "To Darnassus". He set out southward along the edge of the vale and passed briefly under the shadow of Timbermaw Hold in Felwood. On the borders of Moonglade he fell, and it was not dwelt upon, for he soon rose again and went on. His road ran into Ashenvale, through Astranaar and by Maestra's Post, and then north into Darkshore. He passed Twilight Vale, Twilight Shore, The Long Wash and Auberdine, and came to Mist's Edge. At each new country he paused to take a picture, as though marking the stones of a long way. From there he crossed The Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village in Teldrassil.

He climbed from Rut'theran into Darnassus and came to the Cenarion Enclave, and there the errand "To Darnassus" was laid down, complete. His business in the city was finished. He went down again to Rut'theran Village and back over The Veiled Sea to the shores of Darkshore, past Mist's Edge, to Auberdine. He travelled alone throughout, and no one shared his road. He grew no greater in strength that night. Still, he had won the blessing of the Great Cat Spirit, and the "Trial of the Sea Lion" was still in his keeping. At Auberdine he rested, with the sea behind him and the long roads of Kalimdor before him.
