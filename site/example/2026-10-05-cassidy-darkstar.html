Chapter 11 — The Statuette of Menethil

Cassidy Darkstar · October 5, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out from Darnassus to Nighthaven in Moonglade, and a picture was taken as he came into that land. There he took up the Trial of the Lake, and it led him by Lake Elune'ara to the Shrine of Remulos, where he saw it through. The Trial of the Sea Lion followed. In the Stormrage Barrow Dens he found the three relics asked of him, the Relic of the Silent Shadow, the Relic of the Fang and the Relic of the Claw, and so ended the errand of The Great Cat Spirit. He carried the Blessings of the Great Cat Spirit back to Nighthaven. From there he was sent on To Darnassus, and the road ran long: by Timbermaw Hold, where he fell once and rose again, then Astranaar and Maestra's Post in Ashenvale, and down through Twilight Vale to Auberdine in Darkshore. He crossed the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and climbed to the Cenarion Enclave, and there that errand was done.

From Auberdine he took ship to Stormwind Harbor. In The Canals he fell in with Rambleon, a hunter, who was his companion on the road for the greater part of all that followed. They went together through the Trade District and the Valley of Heroes, by Goldshire into Northshire Valley, and in the Library Wing of the Main Hall the errand of Brother Paxton was finished and Ink Supplies taken in hand. Then they turned back to Ashenvale, past the Shrine of Aessina to Maestra's Post, where Cassidy took up Supplies to Auberdine. On the road north he overcame Dark Strand Assassins and Forsaken Scouts, and with them Balizar the Umbrage, Caedakar the Vicious and Aligar the Tormentor. At The Master's Glaive Twilight Thugs and Twilight Disciples fell to him, and he passed on by Remtravel's Excavation. On The Zoram Strand he met Wrathtail Sorceresses and Wave Riders, a Mystlash Hydra and a Spined Crawler. Then he went down into Blackfathom Deeps, among Murkshallow Snapclaws and Blindlight Murlocs, as far as The Drowned Sacellum, and many pictures were taken in those depths.

Afterwards the road bent south over the water to Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands, where errands came thick upon him, among them Claws from the Deep, Young Crocolisk Skins, Spoils of War and In Search of the Excavation Team. He hunted crocolisks, young and grown and giant, through Menethil Bay and Black Channel Marsh until the skins were gathered. He gathered Khaz Modan Timber and Khaz Modan Iron at Baradin Bay and Menethil Bay and brought them to Menethil Keep. In Bluegill Marsh he cut down the Bluegill Murlocs and took the head of Gobbler. Reclaiming Goods and The Search Continues were completed there, and in that marsh Zogg, a shaman, joined them for a short while. In Bluegill Marsh Cassidy came into new strength and reached his twenty-third level, and a picture marked it.

Search More Hovels gave up the Menethil Statuette. Soon afterwards Rambleon and Zogg went their own ways, and Cassidy walked on alone. He brought the statuette to Karl Boran, and then to Captain Stoutfist in Menethil Keep, who gave it back into his hands with a further charge, A Friend of the Family: to bear it to Highlord Bolvar Fordragon in Stormwind Keep. He took up War Banners and From the Ashes as well, and at the last he came to rest in the Deepwater Tavern. The statuette was still in his keeping, and the way to Stormwind Keep lay open before him.
