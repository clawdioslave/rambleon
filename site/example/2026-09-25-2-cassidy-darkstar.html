Chapter 3 — The Barrow Den and the Tree's Errand

Cassidy Darkstar · September 25, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar began in The Oracle Glade, and there he was not long alone, for Yuuki the warrior and Mil the priest joined him. Yuuki kept company with him only for that first stretch, but Mil stayed longest of all who came that night. Together they overcame Witchmother Arysa and took her amulet, and so the errand called "Darkness in the Glade" was ended. Then came "The Oracle Tree," quickly done, and from it a heavier charge: Cassidy was to bear the Inscribed Bark to Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm in Darnassus. Before he left the glade he slew Lady Sathrah and took her Silvery Spinnerets for the errand named "Tears of the Moon."

He went up into Darnassus, passing the Warrior's Terrace, where he took up the errand "Nessa Shadowsong," and so came to the Cenarion Enclave. There he gave the bark into Fandral's keeping, and in return was given a message for Onu in the Grove of the Ancients, far off in Darkshore, south of Auberdine. That was a road for another day. In the Temple of the Moon he laid down the spinnerets, and was given a new charge, "Sathrah's Sacrifice," before he turned back down into Teldrassil by the Pools of Arlithrien and Lake Al'Ameth.

Much of what followed happened in Ban'ethil Hollow and the Barrow Den beneath it, where the Gnarlpine were many. Shivv the hunter walked with him for a while, and with Shivv he won the Shaman Voodoo Charm that finished "The Sleeping Druid." After that he took up "Druid of the Claw." The Barrow Den was hard ground and he fell there more than once, but each time he came back to it. When Shivv and Mil went their ways, Elundra the warrior, Tina the priest and Justín the hunter joined him, and Justín stayed with him through most of the rest of the road. Amid the Rageclaws and the Webwood Venomfangs Cassidy reached his twelfth level and came into new strength, and a picture was taken at that moment. Victoza, a druid like himself, joined him then and kept faith with him for a good while. Together they saw "Druid of the Claw" and "Escaping Ban'ethil" through, and by Lake Al'Ameth Cassidy finished "The Shimmering Frond" and "The Sprouted Fronds" as well.

Further south lay Gnarlpine Hold. There Elora the priest stood beside him only briefly, and Ursal the Mauler was slain. Cassidy brought word of it back to Dolanaar and passed through Starbreeze Village. When Victoza had parted from him too, he climbed once more to the Temple of the Moon and ended "Sathrah's Sacrifice." All told, Gnarlpine shamans, defenders, warriors and augurs had fallen before him by the score that night.

Last of all he came down to Rut'theran Village, and with that every part of Teldrassil had been walked and was known to him. The message for Onu was still in his keeping, and the road to Darkshore lay open.
