Chapter 10 — The Doors of Blackfathom

Cassidy Darkstar · October 3, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar's road began again upon The Zoram Strand in Ashenvale, and from that first step it ran towards the old waters of Blackfathom Deeps. It was there that he first fell in with Errook, a druid like himself and a follower of the same quiet craft. Their fellowship was the shortest on the road. Together they passed into Blackfathom Deeps, and Cassidy marked that threshold with a picture so that he would remember it, but soon after crossing it the two went their separate ways. No tale records what parted them, only that they did part, as travellers sometimes do.

Then Cassidy turned back. Once more he stood upon The Zoram Strand, and once more he passed the doors of Blackfathom Deeps, as a man returns to a door he has not finished with. On this second going-in he was joined by Bluntz, a paladin, and the two of them walked the Deeps together for a good stretch of the way. Later a third came to them: Rambleon, a hunter, who joined their company in those dark halls. So there were three now where there had been one, and the fellowship held together, though no record tells what they faced in the Deeps, or what they came looking for.

There in Blackfathom Deeps, Cassidy fell, and his road brought him next to Twilight Vale in Darkshore. He marked his coming into that land with a second picture, as he had marked the doors of the Deeps before. He did not stay long among the fallen. He was soon back among the living, and that is the part of the tale worth keeping. Not long after, Bluntz parted from him, the paladin taking his own road as Errook had done before him. Of Rambleon the record speaks no farewell.

It had been a night of thresholds more than of victories. Cassidy found no new strength, took up no errand and brought none to an end, and he carried away no treasure from the dark water. Still, three lands had felt his tread: The Zoram Strand, Blackfathom Deeps and Twilight Vale. Three companions had walked beside him, some only briefly and some for longer, and each had been welcome. Such nights come on any long road, and they belong to the tale as much as the great ones do. Cassidy Darkstar stood in Darkshore with the paths of Ashenvale behind him, and the road still lay open before him.
