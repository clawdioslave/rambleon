Chapter 10 — The Road to the Deeps and Back

Cassidy Darkstar · October 3, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar came first to The Zoram Strand in Ashenvale, and there he was joined by Errook, a druid like himself, so that for a little while two of the same craft walked together. Their fellowship was brief. They went down together into Blackfathom Deeps, and at the threshold Cassidy stopped to take a picture, as a traveller might mark a door he means to remember. Soon after that, Errook went his own way, and Cassidy was alone again on the edge of that old and sunken place.

He did not turn from it. He came out once more upon The Zoram Strand and then went back down into Blackfathom Deeps, as though the place still had some claim on him. This time he did not walk alone for long. Bluntz, a paladin, joined him, and not long after came Rambleon, a hunter. The three went on together into the Deeps for a time. Within those halls Cassidy fell. It is told here once and plainly. When he came to himself it was in Twilight Vale in Darkshore, far to the north, and there too he took a picture as he entered. Bluntz, who had been his companion for a fair stretch of that venture, parted from him there.

After a long pause in the tale, Cassidy came again to Twilight Vale, and there Rambleon found him once more. Of all who shared the road with him in this chapter, the hunter stayed longest, and his return was a kindness worth setting down. From the Vale Cassidy turned south again into Ashenvale, and he marked his entering of that land with a third picture. Then he came to Bathran's Haunt, a name that sounds as though it carries some older story, though the record does not tell it.

No errands were taken up or finished in this chapter, and no foe gave up its strength to him. Cassidy Darkstar ended as he began, in the twenty-first year of his craft. Still, he had walked from the Strand to the Deeps and from Darkshore back to the forests of Ashenvale, and he had found three companions on the way, each in his turn. He had seen Bathran's Haunt for the first time. At the last he stood again in Twilight Vale, with the roads south and north both open before him.
