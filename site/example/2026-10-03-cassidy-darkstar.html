Chapter 10 — The Companions at Blackfathom Deeps

Cassidy Darkstar · October 3, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up his road again on The Zoram Strand in Ashenvale, and he did not travel alone for long. Errook, a druid like himself and of the same old craft of leaf and claw, joined him there. Together they passed into Blackfathom Deeps, a name that has long been spoken with care in that country. Cassidy marked the moment of their entering with a picture, as a traveller keeps a pressed leaf between the pages of a book. But the fellowship with Errook was a brief one. They had hardly crossed the threshold together before their paths divided, and Cassidy was left alone at the door of the Deeps.

He did not turn back. He stepped out onto The Zoram Strand once more and then went down into Blackfathom Deeps again, as one who has made up his mind and will not be put off. Before long a new companion came to him: Bluntz, a paladin, who stood beside him as the errand took shape. Some while later Rambleon the hunter joined them, and so the three went on together, druid and paladin and hunter. Such meetings are small things in the long tale of a road. Still they are worth remembering, for no one goes far into deep places without good company.

Within the Deeps Cassidy fell, and that was all there was to it. The road took him next to Twilight Vale in Darkshore, and there he took a second picture to mark his coming. There, too, he returned among the living, as those of his kind may, and he set his feet once more on solid ground. Soon afterward Bluntz took his leave, and the fellowship that had gathered at Blackfathom Deeps came apart as such fellowships do. No ill came of it. It was simply that each traveller had his own road to follow.

It was a short chapter and a quiet one, with no new strength gained, no errands finished, and no spoils carried away. Yet Cassidy had passed twice through the doors of Blackfathom Deeps, and he had walked for a time with Errook, with Bluntz, and with Rambleon, whose names now belong to his story. He rested at last in Twilight Vale. Behind him lay the Strand and the Deeps, and ahead of him the road ran on through Darkshore toward whatever errands were still to come.
