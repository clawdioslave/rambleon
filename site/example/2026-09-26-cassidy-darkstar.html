Chapter 4 — The Tower of Althalaxx

Cassidy Darkstar · September 26, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Auberdine into the wider lands of Darkshore, and there the Thistle Bears came against him first. Two travellers joined him on that road: Shcat, a druid of his own calling, who walked with him only briefly before turning aside, and Istvanna the hunter, who stayed at his side much longer. Together they went on among the Rabid Thistle Bears and the Moonstalkers, by Cliffspring River and the stones of Bashal'Aran, until twenty of the maddened bears had fallen and the errand called the Cleansing of the Infected was fulfilled. Cassidy took more than one picture along the way, as though he knew these doings were worth remembering.

Beyond the river lay Cliffspring Falls, where the Stormscale Sirens and the Stormscale Wave Riders held the water. There Pheonix, another hunter, came into the company for a short while. Among the Twilight Thugs Cassidy sought the Death Cap and then the Scaber Stalks for the errand of the Cave Mushrooms. It was stubborn ground, and he fell there more than once, but each time he returned and pressed on until all five stalks were gathered. After that Pheonix and Istvanna went their own ways. Cassidy drew his sample from the Cliffspring River, came to Mist's Edge, and saw the first part of the business of the Tower of Althalaxx done, and took up the next part.

Then he returned to Auberdine, and there many threads were tied off together. He gave account of the cleansing, the mushrooms and the river's water, and in doing so he came into new strength and reached his fifteenth level, and a picture was taken to mark it. New errands were laid upon him: Tharnariun's Hope, Onu, WANTED: Murkdeep!, Fruit of the Sea, The Absent Minded Prospector and The Blackwood Corrupted. Rambleon, a hunter, joined him in Auberdine and travelled with him for the longest stretch of all his companions.

With Rambleon beside him, Cassidy went north again to the Tower of Althalaxx, where the Dark Strand Fanatics fell before him one after another. He fell once within the tower's bounds, but he came back and went on to the Ruins of Mathystra. There Delmanis the Hated was overcome, and the four Worn Parchments were recovered. He took pictures at the tower and among the ruins as if to fix the deed in memory. With the parchments delivered, the errand turned outward: Balthule's letter was to be carried to Delgren the Purifier in Ashenvale Forest. Yet Cassidy first turned home. He passed through Auberdine, The Long Wash and Mist's Edge and over The Veiled Sea to Teldrassil, came up through Rut'theran Village, and entered Darnassus, and he took a picture at each crossing. He walked Warrior's Terrace, Tradesmen's Terrace and Craftsmen's Terrace, and came at last to the Cenarion Enclave. He stood there with the letter still in his keeping, and Ashenvale Forest lay somewhere south along the road.
