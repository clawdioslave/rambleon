Chapter 4 — The Cleansing of the Thistle Bears

Cassidy Darkstar · September 26, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Auberdine and went into the wide lands of Darkshore. Before he had gone far, a Thistle Bear fell to him, and he took a picture there to mark the start of the road. Soon after, two companions joined him. One was Shcat, a druid of his own calling, who kept company with him only briefly before going her own way. The other was Istvanna, a hunter, who walked beside him longer than any other he met on that road. Together they began the grim work of "Cleansing of the Infected." The first of the Rabid Thistle Bears fell, then the Moonstalkers, and the path led on to Cliffspring River and to Bashal'Aran, where a Moonstalker Runt was brought down.

The bears were hunted one after another until twenty of them lay slain and that errand was fulfilled. Then Cassidy followed the river up to Cliffspring Falls. There the Stormscale Sirens and the Stormscale Wave Riders came against him, and a third companion joined, Pheonix the hunter. At the Falls Cassidy found the Death Cap he had been sent for, and a Twilight Thug fell before him. The ground was hard, and he fell there once, but he rose and went back to the Falls. He gathered the five Scaber Stalks that the errand of "Cave Mushrooms" required, and he took a picture at the Falls and again beside the river. At Cliffspring River the fellowship broke. Pheonix and Istvanna went their ways, and Cassidy went on alone.

He did not linger. He drew a sample from the waters of Cliffspring River, as "The Cliffspring River" had asked of him, and went north to Mist's Edge. There he saw "The Tower of Althalaxx" through its first stage and took up its next charge, for that tale was not yet finished. Then he turned back and went down again by the river road to Auberdine. There he gave account of the bears and the mushrooms and the river's water. In return he received new errands, one after another: "Tharnariun's Hope," "Onu," "WANTED: Murkdeep!," "Fruit of the Sea," "The Absent Minded Prospector," and "The Blackwood Corrupted." Many of them pointed back into the lands he had just walked.

In Auberdine, too, Cassidy came into new strength and reached his fifteenth level, and he took a picture of that moment. The hunting of that day had been long. The Stormscale Wave Riders and Sirens, the Rabid Thistle Bears and the Moonstalkers had fallen to him by the score, and his hand had grown steadier for it. Near the end another hunter, Rambleon, joined him. Cassidy looked out once more over the shores of Darkshore with his errands gathered about him and a new companion at his side, and the road ran on before them.
