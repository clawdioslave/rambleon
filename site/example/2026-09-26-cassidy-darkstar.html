Chapter 4 — The Tower and the Moonlit Glade

Cassidy Darkstar · September 26, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Auberdine into the wilds of Darkshore. The Thistle Bears fell before him first, and then the Rabid Thistle Bears, twenty of them in all, until the Cleansing of the Infected was done. Shcat, a druid of his own calling, walked beside him only a little way. Istvanna the hunter stayed longer, and at Cliffspring Falls Pheonix, also a hunter, joined them. Together they fought the Stormscale Sirens and Wave Riders and gathered the Death Cap and the Scaber Stalks for the errand of the Cave Mushrooms. That road was not easy, and he fell there more than once. But the mushrooms were gathered all the same, and water was drawn from the Cliffspring River.

When he returned to Auberdine his labours were answered, and he came into new strength. There he took up many errands at once: Tharnariun's Hope, Onu, Fruit of the Sea, The Absent Minded Prospector, The Blackwood Corrupted, and the bounty of WANTED: Murkdeep. Rambleon the hunter joined him, and of all his companions on this road he stayed the longest and was the most faithful. Together they climbed to the Tower of Althalaxx, where the Dark Strand Fanatics fell in their numbers. In the Ruins of Mathystra they overcame Delmanis the Hated and recovered four Worn Parchments. After that, Balthule's letter was given into Cassidy's keeping, to be carried to Delgren the Purifier in Ashenvale Forest.

Then Cassidy crossed the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and went up into Darnassus. In the Cenarion Enclave he took up Lessons Anew. He returned to Twilight Shore and fought the Greymist folk: seers, netters, warriors and hunters. There Caerwyn the paladin and Reighna the hunter stood with him for a short while, and together they struck down Murkdeep. On the shore he found the bones of a Beached Sea Creature and a Strangely Marked Box, which he carried back to Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde in Auberdine. Elentria, a paladin, walked with him briefly on that shore. From Auberdine he took up the Trek to Ashenvale, and then he came for the first time to Moonglade and to Nighthaven. There he answered the Great Bear Spirit and completed Lessons Anew. After that his road ran back and forth: to Darnassus for Body and Heart, and down into Darkshore, where a Lunaclaw was overcome. Then he went once more to the Enclave, where that trial was ended.

Last of all he went to Ameth'Aran and read the Lay of Ameth'Aran and the Fall of Ameth'Aran, and in doing so he had walked all of Darkshore. Cursed, Writhing and Wailing Highborne fell before him. Viserion the druid, Soy the rogue and Duncard the paladin shared his road for a moment only, and then went their own ways. When the seven Highborne Relics were gathered and the Tools of the Highborne were delivered in Auberdine, he came into strength once more. A picture was taken to mark it. At Mist's Edge he took three eyes from the Darkshore Threshers, completing Buzzbox 411, and took up Buzzbox 323. Balthule's letter was still in his keeping, and Ashenvale lay before him.
