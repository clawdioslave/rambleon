Chapter 4 — The Tower of Althalaxx and the Lay of Ameth'Aran

Cassidy Darkstar · September 26, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out from Auberdine into Darkshore, and the Thistle Bears fell before him. Twenty of the Rabid Thistle Bears were brought down, and so the errand called the Cleansing of the Infected was fulfilled. Shcat, a druid, walked beside him only briefly. Istvanna the hunter kept company with him longer, and at Cliffspring Falls Pheonix, also a hunter, joined them. There he went among the Stormscale Sirens and Wave Riders, and he gathered the Death Cap and the Scaber Stalks for Cave Mushrooms. Then he drew a sample from the Cliffspring River. That stretch of road was not kind to him, and he fell there, but every errand was carried through all the same.

Then he climbed to the Tower of Althalaxx, where the Dark Strand Fanatics stood against him. Beyond it, in the Ruins of Mathystra, Delmanis the Hated was overcome and the four Worn Parchments were recovered. Pictures were taken there. After that Balthule's letter was given into his keeping, to be carried at last to Delgren the Purifier in Ashenvale Forest. Back in Auberdine he came into the strength of his fifteenth level. He took up new errands, among them Tharnariun's Hope, Onu, and the hunt for Murkdeep. There also Rambleon the hunter joined him. Of all who shared the road with him, Rambleon stayed the longest, and he returned again after a parting.

He crossed the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and Darnassus, and in the Cenarion Enclave the task named Lessons Anew was set before him. Then he went south along the Twilight Shore among the Greymist, the Tide Crawlers and the Reef Crawlers. There Caerwyn the paladin and Reighna the hunter stood with him while Murkdeep was slain. Elentria, a paladin, joined him briefly afterward, and the bones of a beached sea creature and a strangely marked box were brought home to Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde. Next the road led to Nighthaven in Moonglade, where he sought the Great Bear Spirit. Then he went back to Darnassus, and afterward to the matter of Body and Heart, which ended with the Lunaclaw overcome.

Last of all he came to Ameth'Aran. He read the Lay of Ameth'Aran and then its Fall, and he contended with the Cursed, Writhing and Wailing Highborne. For a little while Viserion the druid, Soy the rogue and Duncard the paladin went with him. Seven Highborne Relics were gathered, and with that the whole of Darkshore had been walked. In Auberdine the Tools of the Highborne were delivered, and he came into his sixteenth level. At Mist's Edge he took three eyes from the Darkshore Threshers for Buzzbox 411, and then he took up Buzzbox 323. The letter for Ashenvale was still in his pack, and the Trek to Ashenvale was among his errands. He was still on the road at Mist's Edge when this chapter closed.
