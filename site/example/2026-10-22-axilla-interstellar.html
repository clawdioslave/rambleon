Chapter 5 — The Summons of Desperate Times

Axilla Interstellar · October 22, 2026

Axilla Interstellar, hunter of the Windshapers and of the Skyborne, took up her road again in Valanaar upon Zephras Isle. It was a hard road that she found there. Again and again her way led out of Valanaar into the Gustberry Lowlands and back, the way one returns to a door that will not open, and each time she came back to the same task. Once at least she fell in Valanaar. But she rose and went on, and in the end the hard ground was not what mattered in this part of her tale.

In Valanaar she met an Ornery Galestrider and overcame it. It was the first of that kind to fall to her bow, and she stopped there to take a picture of the moment, so that it would not be lost. Then she turned to the errand called Desperate Times, and the name was true to the need. Its first charge was to recruit the Windshapers, her own kin, and she saw it through. Later in Valanaar she carried out the second charge and recruited the High Order. Both summons were answered, and the errand that had begun in need was now well on its way.

After this her road bent toward the Gustberry Lowlands. She met trouble there as well, but she did not turn aside from it. She came back through Valanaar and crossed the Lowlands again, and so she reached the East Pylon Watchtower. No one had set her feet on that ground before. She stayed some while at the Watchtower before going down again into the Lowlands. There, as her labours drew to a close, she faced a Vuldren Alpha and slew it, and it was the first of its kind to fall before her. It was the greatest of her deeds on that road. Before she rested, she took one more picture in the Gustberry Lowlands.

She gained no new strength that night, and she was no higher in her craft at the end than at the beginning. Still, she had seen two summons through and walked three places of Zephras Isle, one of them new to her. Two beasts that had never fallen to her before now had. She had gone alone and kept on alone, and she had not given up her errand. The Watchtower now lay behind her and the Lowlands around her, and the road ran on beyond them across the isle.
