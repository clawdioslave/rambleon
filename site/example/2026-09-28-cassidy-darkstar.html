Chapter 6 — The Pridewings and the Fossil

Cassidy Darkstar · September 28, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again beside Mirkfallon Lake in the Stonetalon Mountains. There Rambleon the hunter joined him, and Rambleon would walk with him through the greater part of all that followed. Cassidy fell once before the work was well begun, but he rose and returned to it. Many a Pridewing Wyvern and Pridewing Consort was brought down along the lake, and a Blackened Basilisk besides, and often he paused to take a picture, as though he wished to keep the place. Then he climbed to Stonetalon Peak, where a Sap Beast fell. There the errand called "Journey to Stonetalon Peak" was ended.

From the heights he passed by the Zoram Strand into Ashenvale, by Lake Falathim and Maestra's Post, to Astranaar. There "Pridewings of Stonetalon" was accounted done, and Cassidy came into the strength of his nineteenth level. He went north then into Darkshore, to Auberdine and down to Remtravel's Excavation. Raín, a hunter, and Is, a warrior, joined him there for a stretch of the road. Together they overcame Cracked Golems and Stone Behemoths, and Gravelflint Scouts and Geomancers. Then they went down into Blackfathom Deeps on the errand "Knowledge in the Deeps," as far as the Drowned Sacellum, where snapclaws and crustaceans and a Blindlight Murloc stood against them. After that, Raín and Is took their leave.

Cassidy went on to Darnassus and down to Rut'theran Village, and crossed the Veiled Sea to Stormwind Harbor, where Rambleon was with him once more. In Cathedral Square "Expanding Horizons" was finished, and he took up "Making Do": Alexston's Reply, to be carried to Thundris Windweaver in Auberdine. In the Mage Quarter "A Scroll from Mauren" was delivered. So he passed through Goldshire, where "A Fishy Peril" was settled, and into Westfall. At the Jansen Stead Verna Furlbrow gave him her recipe for Westfall Stew to bear to Salma Saldean. A Dust Devil yielded its debris, and at Sentinel Hill he made his report to Gryan Stoutmantle. Around Stendel's Pond the Riverpaw brutes and bandits were thinned by the score.

Then he went to Nighthaven in Moonglade, where "A Lesson to Learn" was ended and "Trial of the Lake" begun, and he came down by Lake Elune'ara. The way back was long. He crossed Darkshore and the Veiled Sea and climbed again to Stonetalon Peak, and so came to Auberdine, where Alexston's reply was given at last. Along the Twilight Shore a Grizzled Thistle Bear and a Greymist Warrior fell before him. At Remtravel's Excavation Val the shaman stood with him only a little while, and Tyu the warrior somewhat longer, and there Rambleon parted from him. Afterwards Daima the priest walked the shore with him. On that shore he came upon the remains of a beached sea turtle and the bones of a sea creature, and gathered the murloc eyes the stew required. The remains and bones he carried to Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde in Auberdine.

Last of all he took a mysterious fossil up through the terraces of Darnassus to Chief Archaeologist Greywhisker. Greywhisker sent it onward, to Archaeologist Flagongut in Menethil Harbor, and the road lay open before him.
