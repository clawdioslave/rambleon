Chapter 19 — The Deeps of Blackfathom

Cassidy Darkstar · October 16, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Nighthaven in Moonglade, passed by Lake Elune'ara down into Darkshore, and went by the Ruins of Mathystra and Mist's Edge over the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and so up into Darnassus. In the Tradesmen's Terrace he took up the errand called Klockmort's Essentials. Then he crossed again to Auberdine, where Bloom of the Heavens was brought to its end. He turned south by the Long Wash and the Twilight Shore into Ashenvale, past Lake Falathim and the Shrine of Aessina to Astranaar and Maestra's Post, and came at last to the Zoram Strand and the mouth of Blackfathom Deeps.

There Nickf, a paladin, became his companion, and soon after Muse, a shaman. Together they went down into the Drowned Sacellum and the Pool of Ask'ar, where Ghamoo-ra was slain, and the Fallenroot and the Blackfathom sea witches gave way before them. Muse parted first and Nickf after. Cassidy had a fall in that dark place, but he went down again. With Uthas the paladin and Elon the mage beside him for a short way, he gathered the last of the corrupted brain stems and bore them to Auberdine, and Researching the Corruption was done.

Then Rambleon, a hunter, joined him, and would walk beside him longer than any other in this chapter. Nebben the rogue kept them brief company. On the Zoram Strand, Dyl and Andy, druids both, and Boozebraid the shaman made up the fellowship, and with them Cassidy went down to the very bottom of the Deeps. Lady Sarevess fell, and the Lorgalis Manuscript was found. In Search of Thaelrid was ended and Blackfathom Villainy begun. Gelihast fell, and Old Serra'kis in the Forgotten Pool. In the Moonshrine Sanctum Twilight Lord Kelris was overcome and his head taken, and in Aku'mai's Lair Aku'mai was slain at the last. Many pictures were taken along that way. The company came out to the Temple of the Moon, and there the three went their ways. On the Craftsmen's Terrace, Blackfathom Villainy was given up, and Cassidy came into the strength of his twenty-eighth level.

His road ran back to Ashenvale, east past Raynewood Retreat into Nightsong Woods. There a Withered Ancient and a Crazed Ancient were overcome and the Iron Shaft recovered for Raene's Cleansing. In the Dor'Danil Barrow Den, Taneel Darkwood and Uthil Mooncall were struck down, as the errand of the Insane Druids required. In Night Run beyond Xavian a Highborne soul was set free. At the Moonwell, Raene's Cleansing was carried forward, and at Maestra's Post the Tower of Althalaxx was carried forward also, with Slowing the Orc Advance and Supplies to Auberdine taken up besides. So he went north once more by Moonglade and Cliffspring River to the Tower of Althalaxx itself. Atoh, Postpartum, Faelen, Karn, Dro and Sylben each stood with him there a little while, and the Dark Strand voidcallers fell. Rambleon parted from him at last beneath the tower. Behind Cassidy lay the Deeps, and before him the road still ran on.
