Chapter 1 — The Road to Darnassus

Cassidy Darkstar · September 24, 2026 · 3h 15m in Azeroth

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Dolanaar at a late hour, and he did not go alone. Rambleon the Hunter joined him at the start, and for some fifty-three minutes they kept the road together. In that time Cassidy laid "Gnarlpine Corruption" and "Denalan's Earth" to rest, took up "The Relics of Wakening" and "Soft Saber Pelts," and went back and forth between the village and the shores of Lake Al'Ameth. The going was hard. He fell three times in the first hour, once beside the lake and twice in the wider wood of Teldrassil, and each time he rose again at Dolanaar and went back out. Fangs of the nightsaber and silk of the webwood were gathered for "Zenn's Bidding." Among the timberlings of Lake Al'Ameth he came into the strength of his sixth year of craft, and a picture was taken at that moment.

With Rambleon beside him he gathered twelve Timberling Sprouts and eight Timberling Seeds, and when those errands were done the two companions parted with courtesy. Cassidy went on alone. He fell once more among the Gnarlpine, and again he was restored at Dolanaar. He filled the Jade Phial at Starbreeze Village for "Crown of the Earth." Near the lake he called on Elune's Light and wrapped himself in Shadowmeld, as "The Goddess Provides" required. Then, for "The Great Outdoors," he sat a while by a campfire, which was perhaps the gentlest act of that whole night. Back in Dolanaar he reached his seventh level, and a second picture was taken.

After that the road led west. At Ban'ethil Hollow he met the Gnarlpine Ambushers, and at the Pools of Arlithrien he filled the Tourmaline Phial. He fell again in the Hollow, came back, completed "Crown of the Earth" once more, and finished "The Road to Darnassus." When he first entered Darnassus a third picture was taken. In the city he completed "The Sisterhood of Elune," then went to the Temple of the Moon, where he accepted "Tears of the Moon." He brought a Timberling Seed to Rellian Greenspyre in the Cenarion Enclave and was given "Tumors" in return.

Beyond the gates Ojoz the Druid joined him briefly, for no more than two minutes. Cassidy fell three times in that short stretch outside the city and each time was brought back within its walls. After that he took up "Eyes of the Sentinels" in earnest and walked the city's terraces and gardens a great many times, until he had found the Craftsman's Terrace Inn, the entrance to the depths of Cenarion Hold, the Darnassus Bank and at last the City Gate. Eight falls stood against him by then, but also two levels and eleven errands completed. In the small hours Cassidy passed out of the gate and came back to Dolanaar, and several quests he had not yet finished were still waiting for him.
