Chapter 18 — The Eggs of the Razormaw

Cassidy Darkstar · October 15, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from Sundown Marsh in the Wetlands, and he had not gone far upon that road before he joined forces with Rambleon, a Hunter, who would be his companion for nearly all of what followed. Together they passed by Ironbeard's Tomb and came up into Saltspray Glen, where the raptors of the highland had their haunts. There the first Highland Raptor fell before them, and soon after the first Highland Scytheclaw. Cassidy took many pictures in that glen, as though he wished to keep some token of each turn of the work.

The errand called "Trying Times" asked for twelve Perfect Razormaw Eggs, and in Saltspray Glen he gathered the full number. Yet the glen was not done with them. The Highland Razormaw came against them often, and twenty-four of that kind were overcome before the end, along with Highland Lashtails and Scytheclaws in plenty. Their wandering led them out to Baradin Bay, where an Elder Razormaw was slain, and then back into the glen again. There Cassidy and Rambleon parted for a brief while, but they joined forces again almost at once, and went on as before. In time the tenth Razormaw Incisor was taken, and the task of "Razormaw Needling" was fulfilled. Among the Fen Creepers that roamed the land the first was slain as well, and that was the beginning of another errand.

Then Cassidy came down out of the glen into the wider Wetlands, where the first Wetlands Crocolisk fell, and he passed by Ironbeard's Tomb once more. There "Razormaw Needling" and "Trying Times" were both delivered and reckoned complete, and a new charge was laid upon him, called "Death to the Dragonmaw." He turned back to Sundown Marsh, and there he and his companion brought down the last of the twelve Fen Creepers that "Blisters on the Land" required. Eleven of those creepers had fallen by their hands before the tally was full. At The Green Belt that errand was given up as done, and two more were taken in its place, "Carnage" and "Horrors in the Highland." So the road gave back as much as it took, and Cassidy left that place with more charges than he had carried there. Fifty-nine foes in all had been overcome since the start, and three errands had been seen through to their end. Cassidy had not grown in strength by any new measure, but he had been steady, and the work had been well done.

From there he came by way of the Deepwater Tavern to Menethil Harbor, and from the harbor he passed at last to Nighthaven in Moonglade, which was a place of his own people. Cassidy took a picture as he entered Moonglade. A little after, he parted from Rambleon, and that was a courteous ending to a long fellowship on the road. The Dragonmaw, the highland, and the carnage he was charged with all remained before him, and the road ran on.
