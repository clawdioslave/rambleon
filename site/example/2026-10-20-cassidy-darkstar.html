Chapter 21 — The Stockade and the Hall of Gears

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor and took the long road through Dun Morogh to the Gates of Ironforge. In Tinker Town he finished Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials and took up new errands, and in the Forlorn Cavern he closed the matter of Knowledge in the Deeps. Then he went south by Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes into Elwynn Forest, and in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind he saw Retrieval for Mauren to its end. In the Redridge Mountains he came to Lakeshire. He found Oslow's toolbox by Lake Everstill and gathered rivets and pikes in the Redridge Canyons for the mending of the Everstill Bridge. At Alther's Mill he slew twelve greater tarantulas and broke their eggs. At Stonewatch Keep he walked a little way with the hunters Aimes, Thordin and Angry, and with Shadow, a druid like himself. He fell there, and turned back.

From Lakeshire he had carried Wiley's Note, and now it led him far. He bore it to Gryan Stoutmantle in Sentinel Tower upon Sentinel Hill, then to Mathias Shaw in the house of SI:7 in Old Town, and back again to Westfall. From there he went down into Moonbrook, where he found a mysterious message, and so that long errand of the Defias Brotherhood was finished. In Kharanos he completed The Day After. He went once into Gnomeregan and slew Addled Lepers there, but he did not stay. He turned instead to Loch Modan. At Algaz Station he delivered Stormpike's goods and recovered the miners' gear from Silver Stream Mine. He found Bingles by The Loch and gathered his scattered tools, the blastencapper, the hammer, the wrench and the screwdriver. He walked every corner of that country until all of Loch Modan was known to him, and he took the ears of tunnel rats for Thelsamar. In Dun Morogh he took wendigo manes in The Grizzled Den and won back stolen blasting powder from Helm's Bed Lake for Gol'Bolar Quarry.

In Stormwind he brought Stormpike's Order to the Dwarven District. Then he went down into the Stormwind Stockade, first beside Blessed the priest for a short while. After that he fought with a stauncher fellowship: Licht the paladin, Kaidie the rogue, Shinon the hunter and Zeno the druid, with Garrett the warrior joining briefly near the end. Together they overcame Targorr the Dread, Kam Deepfury, Hamhock, Bazil Thredd and Dextren Ward, and they quelled the uprising of the prisoners. In Old Town, with the red bandanas of The Color of Blood delivered, Cassidy came into new strength, his thirtieth measure. A picture was taken of that moment. The Hallow's End treats for Jesper sent him onward. He flexed for Innkeeper Allison to earn Stormwind Nougat, then went by Moonglade, Darkshore and Teldrassil to Darnassus, where he danced for Innkeeper Saelienne and was given the Darnassus Marzipan.

Last of all he returned to Gnomeregan. Rambleon the hunter went with him longest of any companion, and with them were Wes the priest and the rogues Banished and Natalya. Maru, a priest, joined them only briefly. Later Ia and Stryker, both hunters, joined the company, and Annie the druid stayed but a moment. They passed through The Clockwerk Run, The Hall of Gears and The Dormitory, and in The Clean Zone Cassidy fed a grime-encrusted object to the Sparklematic 5200. Then he came back to Menethil Harbor, where the road had begun, and the road still ran on before him.
