Chapter 21 — The Bridge, the Brotherhood and the Loch

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor and took the road through Dun Morogh to the Gates of Ironforge, and passed within. In Tinker Town he saw Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials brought to their end, and took up new errands in their place: Data Rescue, The Day After, The Grand Betrayal and Essential Artificials. In the Library he accepted Mythology of the Titans, and in the Forlorn Cavern he laid down Knowledge in the Deeps. Then he turned south. The names of the road went by him one after another, Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes, Elwynn Forest and the Valley of Heroes, until he stood in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind. There Retrieval for Mauren was finished, and in the Wizard's Sanctum he was given Power Overwhelming.

By Goldshire and Eastvale Logging Camp he came into the Redridge Mountains, and there he laboured long for the folk of Lakeshire. He saw Ink Supplies delivered. He found Oslow's Toolbox by Lake Everstill, and the lost tools were returned. For the Everstill Bridge he gathered iron rivets and iron pikes in the Redridge Canyons. At Alther's Mill he slew twelve Greater Tarantulas and broke six of their eggs, and the mill was the quieter for it. At the inn and the town hall he took up the wanted notices for Lieutenant Fangore, Gath'Ilzogg and Incinerator Gar'im, and he was given Wiley's Note in the matter of the Defias Brotherhood. Then he went to Stonewatch Keep. There, for a little while, four others walked beside him: the hunters Aimes, Thordin and Angry, and Shadow, a druid of his own calling. They were brief companions, met and parted with in courtesy, and at the Keep he fell, as even the wary sometimes do.

Then the errand of the Defias Brotherhood carried him far. He bore Wiley's Note to Gryan Stoutmantle at Sentinel Tower in Westfall. From there he took it to Mathias Shaw in SI:7 of Stormwind, and he came back again with Shaw's report. At Moonbrook he found A Mysterious Message and brought it to Sentinel Hill, and there that long errand was ended. By Blackrock Mountain and the Sea of Cinders he went north again to Kharanos. There The Day After was finished and Gnogaine begun. He went down into Gnomeregan and slew three Addled Lepers, and pictures were taken there to mark the venture.

Last of all he crossed North Gate Pass into Loch Modan. At Algaz Station he completed Stormpike's Delivery, and he recovered the miners' gear from Silver Stream Mine for Filthy Paws. By The Loch he found Bingles, and he gathered that one's blastencapper, hammer, wrench and screwdriver where they lay scattered. He walked the land from the Farstrider Lodge to Ironband's Excavation Site, past Mo'grosh Stronghold and on to the Stonewrought Dam. By then all of Loch Modan was known to him, and the deed was counted among his achievements. In Thelsamar he took up new labours, and he reported to Mountaineer Rockgar at Dun Algaz. He gathered twelve tunnel rat ears in the mine and brought them back, and Rat Catching was done. The Algaz Gauntlet and Stonegear's Search still lay in his keeping, and the road went on from Thelsamar.
