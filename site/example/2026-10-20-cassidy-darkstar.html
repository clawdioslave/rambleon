Chapter 21 — The Everstill Bridge

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor, and passing by Menethil Bay he left the Wetlands behind and came into Dun Morogh, and so to the Gates of Ironforge. Within the mountain he went by the Great Forge and the Hall of Explorers to Tinker Town, and there two errands were at last laid down: Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials. New ones were taken up in their place, Data Rescue and The Day After, The Grand Betrayal and Essential Artificials, so that his burden grew lighter and heavier at once. In the Library he accepted Mythology of the Titans, and in the Forlorn Cavern he saw Knowledge in the Deeps brought to its end. He took a picture as he came into Dun Morogh, and another at the threshold of Ironforge.

Then he went out again, past Steelgrill's Depot and Kharanos, and the road ran south through lands whose very names spoke of fire: Searing Gorge, Firewatch Ridge and Blackchar Cave, the Altar of Storms and Draco'dar in the Burning Steppes. Into each he came and out of each he passed, and he kept a picture of several as he went, until Elwynn Forest and Northshire Valley lay about him. He entered Stormwind City by the Valley of Heroes. In the Mage Quarter the errand called Retrieval for Mauren was finished, and in the Wizard's Sanctum he took up Power Overwhelming.

From Stormwind he went by Goldshire and the Eastvale Logging Camp, and came into the Redridge Mountains at Three Corners. There Blackrock Bounty was given him. At Lakeshire by Lake Everstill he completed Ink Supplies and took up Rethban Ore and The Lost Tools. At the Lakeshire Inn he set down one charge of The Defias Brotherhood and received the next, which was to carry Wiley's Note to Gryan Stoutmantle in Westfall. More were pressed upon him in that town, at the inn and the Town Hall: WANTED: Lieutenant Fangore, What Comes Around..., WANTED: Gath'Ilzogg, WANTED: Incinerator Gar'im, and Songblade Search. Small labours followed, and he saw them through. He found Oslow's Toolbox by the lake and returned it. He gathered five Iron Rivets and five Iron Pikes in the Redridge Canyons for The Everstill Bridge. At Alther's Mill he slew twelve Greater Tarantulas and destroyed six of their eggs, and so the mill's errand too was ended.

Last he went by the Lakeridge Highway to Stonewatch, where he fell and rose again. Before Stonewatch Keep he joined company with four others: the hunters Aimes, Thordin and Angry, and Shadow, a druid as he was. That fellowship was brief. Shadow and Thordin went their ways almost as soon as it was made, and Aimes soon after. Angry stayed longest and parted last of all. Cassidy went on at the Keep alone, and afterward he came again to the Deepwater Tavern in the Wetlands, where the road had begun. The errands he still carried, for Westfall and the Keep and the lore of the Titans, lay before him on the road.
