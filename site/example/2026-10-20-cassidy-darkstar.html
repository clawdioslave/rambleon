Chapter 21 — The Stockade and the Halls of Gears

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Deepwater Tavern at Menethil Harbor, and his road ran first to Ironforge. In Tinker Town he brought Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials to their end, and in the Forlorn Cavern beneath the Hall of Explorers he laid down the errand called Knowledge in the Deeps. Then he went south through Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes into Elwynn Forest, and in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind he finished Retrieval for Mauren. Beyond lay the Redridge Mountains. At Lakeshire he recovered Oslow's toolbox, gathered rivets and pikes in the Redridge Canyons for the mending of the Everstill Bridge, and at Alther's Mill he slew the greater tarantulas and broke their eggs. Stonewatch Keep did not yield to him, and he turned from it. For a little while Aimes, Thordin and Angry, hunters all, and Shadow, a druid of his own kind, had stood with him there.

Wiley's Note was a longer errand. Cassidy bore it to Gryan Stoutmantle at Sentinel Hill in Westfall, then to Mathias Shaw in the house of SI:7, and then back again with Shaw's report. At Moonbrook he found a mysterious message, and with it he finished his part in the matter of the Defias Brotherhood. In Kharanos he saw The Day After through. He looked briefly into Gnomeregan and slew the addled lepers there. Afterward he crossed into Loch Modan. At Algaz Station he reclaimed the miners' gear, at the Loch he gathered Bingles' scattered tools, and in Silver Stream Mine he took tunnel rats for Thelsamar. Before he left he had walked the whole of Loch Modan. Back in Dun Morogh he took wendigo manes in the Grizzled Den and recovered the stolen blasting powder for Gol'Bolar Quarry.

In the Dwarven District of Stormwind he delivered Stormpike's Order. Then he went down into the Stormwind Stockade. Blessed, a priest, went in with him at first. After that came Licht the paladin, Kaidie the rogue, Shinon the hunter and Zeno the druid, and that fellowship held together through the long fighting. Garrett, a warrior, joined them only at the end. Together they overcame Targorr the Dread, Kam Deepfury, Hamhock, Bazil Thredd, and at last Dextren Ward, whose hand was taken in answer for his crimes. The uprising was quelled, and the red bandanas were gathered. Cassidy took many pictures in those halls. In Old Town, when the reckoning was made, he came into his thirtieth year of strength, and he took a picture of that too.

There were lighter errands in the season of Hallow's End. He flexed for Innkeeper Allison and earned Stormwind nougat. Then he came to Nighthaven in Moonglade and went on through Darkshore to Darnassus, where he danced for Innkeeper Saelienne and received her marzipan. In Ironforge new companions gathered about him: Rambleon the hunter, Wes the priest, and Banished and Natalya, both rogues. Maru, a priest, joined them only briefly. With the four who remained he went down once more into Gnomeregan, through the Clockwerk Run and into the Hall of Gears, and there he took one more picture. The way ran on into the deep places under the mountain.
