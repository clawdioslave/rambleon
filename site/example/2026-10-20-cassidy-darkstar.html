Chapter 21 — The Bridge at Lakeshire

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out from the Deepwater Tavern, and passing through Menethil Harbor and by Menethil Bay he left the Wetlands behind and came into Dun Morogh, and so by the Gates of Ironforge into the city of the dwarves. A picture was taken at each threshold, as a traveller marks a door he means to remember. In Tinker Town he laid down two errands he had long carried, Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials, and took up others in their place: Data Rescue, The Day After, The Grand Betrayal, and Essential Artificials. In The Library he was given Mythology of the Titans. In The Forlorn Cavern he brought Knowledge in the Deeps to its end. Then he went out again by The Great Forge and the gates.

The road south was long in names. Steelgrill's Depot and Kharanos fell behind him. Then came Searing Gorge, Firewatch Ridge and Blackchar Cave, and after them the Altar of Storms and Draco'dar in the Burning Steppes. At last he came to Northshire Valley in Elwynn Forest and to the Valley of Heroes before Stormwind City. At each new border he paused to take a picture, though what he saw there is his own to keep. In the Mage Quarter he fulfilled Retrieval for Mauren, and in the Wizard's Sanctum he accepted Power Overwhelming. Then by Goldshire and the Eastvale Logging Camp he came to Three Corners, and so into the Redridge Mountains.

There the errands gathered about him thickly. On the road he took up Blackrock Bounty. At Lakeshire he finished Ink Supplies and was given Rethban Ore and The Lost Tools. In the Lakeshire Inn he saw one part of The Defias Brotherhood through and accepted the next, and he was charged to take Wiley's Note to Gryan Stoutmantle in Westfall. More bounties followed: WANTED: Lieutenant Fangore, What Comes Around..., and Songblade Search, and in the Lakeshire Town Hall WANTED: Gath'Ilzogg and WANTED: Incinerator Gar'im. These were heavy names, and he carried them lightly for now. He found Oslow's Toolbox by Lake Everstill and returned it. Then he went up into the Redridge Canyons for the Everstill Bridge and came back with five iron rivets and five iron pikes, and the bridge was the better for them. At Alther's Mill he slew twelve greater tarantulas and destroyed six of their eggs, and the people of Lakeshire were rid of that trouble.

Last he took the Lakeridge Highway east to Stonewatch. He fell there once, as travellers sometimes do, but he rose and went on. At Stonewatch Keep four companions joined him for a short stretch of the road. Shadow, a druid like himself, parted from him almost at once, and Thordin the hunter soon after. Aimes, a hunter, stayed a little longer, and Angry, a hunter also, stayed longest of the four. When they had gone their ways he turned back at length to the Deepwater Tavern in the Wetlands, where he had begun, with nine errands done and many more still in his keeping. The road to Westfall and to the keeps of Redridge lay before him, and he had not yet chosen which way to take.
