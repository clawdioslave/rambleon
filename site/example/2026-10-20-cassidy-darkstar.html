Chapter 21 — The Long Errands of Lakeshire and the Stockade

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar went out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor and took the road to Ironforge. In Tinker Town he saw Castpipe's Task and Klockmort's Essentials to their end, and in the Forlorn Cavern he laid Knowledge in the Deeps to rest. From there he passed south through the Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes into Elwynn Forest, and in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind he completed the Retrieval for Mauren. Then he came into the Redridge Mountains, to Lakeshire upon Lake Everstill. There he recovered Oslow's Toolbox, gathered rivets and pikes in the Redridge Canyons for the Everstill Bridge, and at Alther's Mill slew twelve greater tarantulas and destroyed six of their eggs. At Stonewatch Keep he fell once and rose again. Aimes, Angry, Thordin and Shadow kept him company there for a brief stretch.

Wiley's Note, found in the Lakeshire Inn, set him on a longer errand. He bore it to Gryan Stoutmantle in Sentinel Tower upon Sentinel Hill, then to Mathias Shaw in the house of SI:7 in Old Town, and then carried Shaw's report back again. He went down to Moonbrook and recovered A Mysterious Message, and so the matter of the Defias Brotherhood was carried forward link by link. In Kharanos he finished The Day After and took up Gnogaine, and he made a first descent into Gnomeregan among the addled lepers. After that came Loch Modan. At Algaz Station he delivered Stormpike's goods and cleared the Silver Stream Mine of its Filthy Paws. By the Loch he found Bingles and recovered his blastencapper, hammer, wrench and screwdriver. He walked the whole of that country, from the Farstrider Lodge to Mo'grosh Stronghold and the Stonewrought Dam, until every corner of Loch Modan was known to him. He brought tunnel rat ears to Thelsamar and reported to Mountaineer Rockgar at Dun Algaz. Back in Dun Morogh he took wendigo manes from the Grizzled Den and recovered sixteen measures of Stolen Blasting Powder by Helm's Bed Lake for Gol'Bolar Quarry.

In Stormwind he brought Stormpike's Order to the Dwarven District, and then he went down into the Stormwind Stockade. Blessed the priest went in with him first. After her came Licht the paladin, Kaidie the rogue, Shinon the hunter and Zeno, a druid as he was, and for a short while Garrett the warrior. Together they overcame Targorr the Dread, Kam Deepfury, Hamhock, Bazil Thredd and Dextren Ward. They quelled the uprising, took ten red wool bandanas, and saw Crime and Punishment answered. Afterwards, in Old Town, Cassidy came into his thirtieth level, and a picture was taken to mark it. He showed his strength before Innkeeper Allison for a gift of nougat.

His road then turned to his own people. It ran through Nighthaven in Moonglade, along Darkshore, and across the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village and Darnassus, where he danced for Innkeeper Saelienne and was given marzipan. Returning to Ironforge, he joined Rambleon the hunter, who proved the most faithful companion of all these journeys. With them went Wes, Banished, Natalya and, for a moment, Maru. Into Gnomeregan they went more than once. Later Ia, Stryker and Annie joined them, and together they passed through the Hall of Gears and the Dormitory to the Clean Zone, where the Sparklematic 5200 gave up its secret. At the last Cassidy came again to Menethil Harbor, where the road had begun, and it still ran on before him.
