Chapter 21 — Errands Between the Mountains

Cassidy Darkstar · October 20, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar set out from the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor, and his road ran first to Ironforge. In Tinker Town he saw "Castpipe's Task" and "Klockmort's Essentials" through to their end and took up new labours in their place: "Data Rescue," "The Day After," "The Grand Betrayal" and "Essential Artificials." In the Library he received "Mythology of the Titans," and in the Forlorn Cavern he laid down "Knowledge in the Deeps." Then he passed by Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes into Elwynn Forest and so to Stormwind City, where in the Mage Quarter "Retrieval for Mauren" was finished and in the Wizard's Sanctum "Power Overwhelming" was given into his keeping.

From Goldshire he went east by Eastvale Logging Camp and Three Corners into the Redridge Mountains, and there the errands came thick about him. At Lakeshire he finished "Ink Supplies," recovered Oslow's toolbox from Lake Everstill, and gathered iron rivets and iron pikes in Redridge Canyons for the Everstill Bridge. At Alther's Mill he slew twelve greater tarantulas and broke six of their eggs, and that work too was done. At the inn and the town hall he took up many more: the bounties on Lieutenant Fangore, Gath'Ilzogg and Incinerator Gar'im, "What Comes Around...," "Songblade Search," and Wiley's note for Gryan Stoutmantle. At Stonewatch Keep four companions joined him for a little way: Angry and Aimes, hunters both, who walked with him longest, and Thordin the hunter and Shadow the druid, whose company was very brief. He fell once in that keep, and after it he turned back to Menethil.

Then the matter of the Defias Brotherhood drew him on. He bore Wiley's note to Gryan Stoutmantle in the Sentinel Tower upon Sentinel Hill, carried it again to Mathias Shaw in SI:7 in Old Town, brought Shaw's report back to Westfall, and at Moonbrook he found a mysterious message, which he delivered to the tower to close that long errand. In Kharanos "The Day After" was finished and "Gnogaine" begun, and he went down into Gnomeregan, as far as the Train Depot, where three addled lepers fell to him. There he paused to take pictures, as he had done at many gates and borders along the way.

Loch Modan received him next. At Algaz Station he finished "Stormpike's Delivery" and "Filthy Paws," gathering the miners' gear from Silver Stream Mine. By the shore of The Loch he found Bingles and recovered his blastencapper, hammer, wrench and screwdriver. He walked every part of that land, from the Farstrider Lodge to Mo'grosh Stronghold and the Stonewrought Dam, until all of Loch Modan was known to him. At Thelsamar he gathered tunnel rat ears for "Rat Catching," and at Dun Algaz he took up "The Algaz Gauntlet." Returning to Dun Morogh, he finished "Stonegear's Search," took eight wendigo manes in the Grizzled Den, and recovered sixteen measures of stolen blasting powder at Helm's Bed Lake. At last he came again to Stormwind, where he set Stormpike's order in the hands of Furen Longbeard in the Dwarven District, and in Cathedral Square he took up "Hallow's End Treats for Jesper." There, within the walls of the city, he paused, and the many roads he had not yet finished lay open before him.
