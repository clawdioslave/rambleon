Chapter 16 — From the Dagger Hills to the Deeps of Blackfathom

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again at Demont's Place in Westfall and went south into The Dagger Hills. There the first of the Defias Highwaymen fell before him, and soon after he saw the errand called "Thunderbrew" to its end. Lucia, a Paladin, joined him in the hills. Together they brought down the fifteen Highwaymen that "The People's Militia" required. They walked side by side as far as the gates of The Deadmines, and there she parted from him. Вольда, a Hunter, kept him company for only a few steps of the way down. In those deep ways Cassidy met Abbigale, a Hunter, who went with him through the greater part of the mine. Later Oldschool, another Hunter, joined them for its last passages. The mine did not yield easily, and once or more Cassidy had to find his way back into it. Yet Undead Dynamiters and an Undead Excavator were overcome, and four Miners' Union Cards were gathered for "Collecting Memories." Foreman Thistlenettle was slain, and his badge was taken for "Oh Brother..." Then the companions went their separate ways.

From Sentinel Tower he gave his account of "The People's Militia." He then passed by Saldean's Farm, touched the edge of Duskwood at The Darkened Bank, and went up through Elwynn Forest to Stormwind City. In Old Town he took up "The Color of Blood." In the Dwarven District he laid down "Oh Brother..." and "Collecting Memories," and there he came into greater strength, reaching his twenty-fifth level. That deed was marked with the name of Novice Druid and with a picture taken. He accepted "Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators," looked briefly into the Stormwind Stockade, and turned east into Elwynn once more. At the Tower of Azora he finished "Further Concerns." Beside Stone Cairn Lake he found the lost guards and learned what had become of Rolf. He carried the medallions of Rolf and Malakai to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. Near Ridgepoint Tower he drove off young forest bears and prowlers to protect the frontier. In Goldshire he delivered Thomas' report. After that he brought the Stormwind Armor Marker to Sara Timberlain and gathered six red linen bandanas in the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch.

He came next to the Deepwater Tavern and Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands. There he took up "Lost in the Thicket Things" and "Fall of Dun Modr," and passed through Bluegill Marsh and Sundown Marsh to the Angerfang Encampment. Slyra, a Warrior, stood with him there for the space of one hard errand. The Dragonmaw raiders and bonewarders fell, "Nek'rosh's Gambit" was finished, and "Defeat Nek'rosh" was taken up. In Mosshide Fen he dealt patiently with gnoll and mongrel until "Tramping Paws" was done. He took the gland of a Leech Stalker for "Alchemical Hazards," and he paused there often to take pictures. At Dun Modr he received "The Dark Iron War." He stood within Ironbeard's Tomb, and then he returned to Menethil.

From Baradin Bay he crossed The Great Sea by the strands of Hillsbrad and came to The Long Wash, Auberdine and Twilight Shore. Then he went on into Ashenvale, past Lake Falathim and the Shrine of Aessina, to Astranaar. There he was given Shindrell's Note to carry to Kayneth Stillwind in Forest Song. But first he went down into Blackfathom Deeps with Needsmo, a Druid of his own craft, and Broven, a Paladin. Together they overcame satyrs, tide priestesses and oracles. At the last Cassidy stood again beneath the roof of the Deepwater Tavern, and the note for Forest Song was still in his keeping.
