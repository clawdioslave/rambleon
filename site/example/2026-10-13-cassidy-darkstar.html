Chapter 16 — The Eye of Paleth

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again in the Dagger Hills of Westfall. There he saw the errand called Thunderbrew to its end, and with Lucia, a paladin who walked with him a short while, he slew the fifteen Defias Highwaymen that The People's Militia required. He went down into The Deadmines next. The dark there cost him dearly more than once, but Abbigale and Oldschool, hunters both, came to stand beside him. Together they gathered the Miners' Union Cards and felled Foreman Thistlenettle, whose badge Cassidy bore away. He came back to Sentinel Tower and reported the militia's work done. Then he went on by Elwynn Forest into Stormwind City, and in the Dwarven District he laid down the badge and the cards. There he came into new strength, and the name of Novice Druid was given to him.

Elwynn still had need of him. At the Tower of Azora he took up Further Concerns and went to find the lost guards. By Stone Cairn Lake he learned what had befallen Rolf, and he carried the medallions of Rolf and Malakai to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. He thinned the young bears and prowlers about Ridgepoint Tower, which was the errand called Protect the Frontier. He bore Thomas' report to Goldshire, brought the armor marker to Sara Timberlain, and gathered the red linen bandanas from the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch. When these small duties were done, he passed through the Deepwater Tavern to Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands.

In the Wetlands his errands came thick and hard. At Angerfang Encampment, with Slyra the warrior at his side, he saw Nek'rosh's Gambit through and took up the charge to defeat Nek'rosh. In Mosshide Fen he fell once among the gnolls, but he rose and went on until both mongrels and gnolls were broken and Tramping Paws was fulfilled. He drew the Unruptured Stalker Gland from a leech stalker for the errand called Alchemical Hazards. He climbed to Dun Modr and learned of its fall, and before he turned west to the harbor he took up The Dark Iron War and walked within Ironbeard's Tomb.

He then went over the sea to his own people's shores. He passed Auberdine and came to Astranaar, where he was given Shindrell's note for Kayneth Stillwind. In Blackfathom Deeps he fought for a while beside Needsmo, a druid like himself, and Broven the paladin. Afterward Rambleon the hunter joined him, and Rambleon kept faith with him longer than any other companion on that road. Together they went a wide way round, through Theramore Isle and the Barrens, Mulgore and Desolace and the Stonetalon Mountains, before they came back into Ashenvale. At The Dor'Danil Barrow Den Cassidy took the Bottle of Disease from the Forsaken. At Forest Song he delivered the note, and there he took up Vengeful Trail and Insane Druids. In Satyrnaar he freed a Highborne soul, as the Tower of Althalaxx had asked of him, and from there he pressed on into Xavian.

The last of his deeds were done at The Lost Fleet. Rambleon was there again, with Blinkín the mage, Arrow the hunter and Pansar the paladin, each for a share of the work. He lifted the curse of the cursed crew, slew Captain Halyndor and won the Intrepid Strongbox Key. When that was done he bore the Cursed Eye of Paleth to Glorin Steelbrow. From there he rode by Dun Morogh, the Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes, back into Elwynn Forest. In the Cathedral of Light he gave the Eye into Archbishop Benedictus's keeping, and it was cleansed. Nord'el still waited in his pack for Thundris Windweaver, and the long road west lay open before him.
