Chapter 16 — The Long Road to Forest Song

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up his road again in Westfall, among the slopes of The Dagger Hills, where the Defias Highwaymen kept their watch. There he saw the errand called Thunderbrew to its end. Lucia, a paladin, joined him for a short stretch of that road, and together they made good the charge of The People's Militia until fifteen of the Highwaymen had fallen. Then he went down into The Deadmines. Вольда, a hunter, walked with him only a few steps before their ways parted. Abbigale, a hunter, stayed with him longer in that deep place, and Oldschool, another hunter, came near the end. The Deadmines did not yield easily, and there was a fall in the dark there that Cassidy did not linger over. But the Undead Dynamiters and Excavators were overcome, the four Miners' Union Cards were gathered for Collecting Memories, and Foreman Thistlenettle was slain and his badge taken.

He climbed back to Sentinel Tower and gave his account of the militia's work. Then he passed by Saldean's Farm and the edge of Duskwood, through Elwynn Forest by Mirror Lake, and so into Stormwind City through the Valley of Heroes. In the Dwarven District he laid down the badge for Oh Brother... and the cards for Collecting Memories. There he came into the strength of his twenty-fifth level and earned the title of Novice Druid, and a picture was taken to mark it. He took up The Color of Blood and Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators, and he looked in upon the Stormwind Stockade. Afterwards he gave himself to the small and needful work of eastern Elwynn. At the Tower of Azora he finished Further Concerns. He found the lost guards and learned Rolf's fate at Stone Cairn Lake, and he carried the medallions of Rolf and Malakai to Guard Thomas at the bridge. He cleared the young bears and the prowlers out by Ridgepoint Tower, bore Thomas' Report to Goldshire, brought the armor marker to Sara Timberlain, and gathered red linen bandanas in the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch.

From the Deepwater Tavern in Menethil Harbor he went out into the Wetlands, across Bluegill Marsh and Sundown Marsh to the Angerfang Encampment. Slyra, a warrior, stood with him there for a brief while against the Dragonmaw. Nek'rosh's Gambit was seen through, and the task of Defeat Nek'rosh was laid upon him. In Mosshide Fen he fulfilled Tramping Paws among the Mosshide gnolls and their mongrels, and he took the gland of a Leech Stalker for Alchemical Hazards. He came at last to Dun Modr and ended the errand of its fall. He took up The Dark Iron War and stood a while in Ironbeard's Tomb.

Then Cassidy crossed the sea to Darkshore and passed into Ashenvale, to Astranaar, where he received Shindrell's note for Kayneth Stillwind. In Blackfathom Deeps the druid Needsmo and the paladin Broven fought beside him against satyrs and Tide Priestesses. Rambleon, a hunter, was the companion who stayed longest that day. With him Cassidy took a great wandering road through Theramore Isle, the Barrens, Mulgore, Desolace and the Stonetalon Mountains, and so back into Ashenvale. In The Dor'Danil Barrow Den he won the Bottle of Disease from the Forsaken. At Forest Song he delivered his note and took up Vengeful Trail and Insane Druids. In Satyrnaar he freed the Highborne soul for The Tower of Althalaxx, and he pressed on into Xavian. Last of all, back in Menethil Harbor, he found the Nord'el for Bloom of the Heavens. Now he bore it for Thundris Windweaver in Auberdine, for Sylessa had spoken of a boat that would carry him there. The harbour lay before him, and the road went on.
