Chapter 16 — From the Dagger Hills to Forest Song

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again in the Dagger Hills of Westfall, where the Defias Highwaymen held the ground. There he saw the errand called Thunderbrew to its end, and Lucia the Paladin joined him for a short stretch as the fifteenth highwayman fell for The People's Militia. Then he went down into the Deadmines. Вольда the Hunter walked beside him only briefly. Abbigale, also a Hunter, stayed longer, and Oldschool joined near the end. The Deadmines did not yield easily, and once at least he was turned back to Sentinel Hill and made the long walk again. Yet in the deep places the Undead Dynamiters and the Skeletal Miners were overcome. Four Miners' Union Cards were gathered for Collecting Memories, Foreman Thistlenettle was slain, and his badge was taken for Oh Brother.

He came up out of the dark and went to Sentinel Tower, and there The People's Militia was finished. From there his road ran past the edge of Duskwood and through Elwynn Forest to Stormwind City. In the Dwarven District he gave up both Oh Brother and Collecting Memories, and with them he came into the strength of his twenty-fifth level and was named a Novice Druid. A picture was taken there. Elwynn still had errands for him. At the Tower of Azora he took up the search for the lost guards, and by Stone Cairn Lake he learned what had become of Rolf. He bore Rolf's and Malakai's medallions to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. He thinned the young forest bears and the prowlers about Ridgepoint Tower to Protect the Frontier, and he carried Thomas' report to Goldshire. Sara Timberlain received the Stormwind Armor Marker, and six red linen bandanas were brought back from the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch.

From Menethil Harbor he went east into the Wetlands, through Bluegill Marsh and Sundown Marsh to the Angerfang Encampment. There Slyra the Warrior stood with him for a little while against the Dragonmaw. Nek'rosh's Gambit was completed, and a darker charge was laid on him: Defeat Nek'rosh. In Mosshide Fen the Mosshide Gnolls and Mongrels fell in their number for Tramping Paws, and he cut an unruptured gland from a Leech Stalker for Alchemical Hazards. He went north to Dun Modr, where Fall of Dun Modr was ended and The Dark Iron War was begun. He looked in at Ironbeard's Tomb and then returned to Menethil Harbor.

Then he crossed the Great Sea to Auberdine in Darkshore and went on into Ashenvale. At Astranaar he took Shindrell's Note to carry to Forest Song. At the Zoram Strand he entered Blackfathom Deeps with Needsmo, a druid like himself, and Broven the Paladin. There the Blackfathom Tide Priestesses and Oracles were overcome. Needsmo kept him company a while longer at Menethil. On the water Rambleon the Hunter joined him, and of all his companions Rambleon walked with him the longest. Together they passed Theramore Isle and Northwatch Hold, crossed the Southern Barrens and Mulgore, and went on through Desolace and the Stonetalon Mountains until they came again to Ashenvale. In the Dor'Danil Barrow Den the Forsaken were put down and a Bottle of Disease was taken. At Forest Song he delivered his note to Kayneth Stillwind and gave his account of the Forsaken Diseases. Then he went into Satyrnaar, where he freed a Highborne soul for The Tower of Althalaxx, and from there on to Xavian. Last of all he turned west again under the trees of Nightsong Woods, with Vengeful Trail and Insane Druids still unanswered, and the road went on before him.
