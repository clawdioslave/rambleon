Chapter 16 — The Medallions and the Long Road

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again in Westfall, in The Dagger Hills below Demont's Place, where the Defias Highwaymen kept their haunts. There he finished the errand called Thunderbrew. Lucia, a paladin, joined him for a short stretch of the way, until the fifteen highwaymen that The People's Militia asked for had fallen. Together they went down into The Deadmines. Вольда the hunter walked with them only a few steps before the company parted. Then Abbigale, a hunter, came to Cassidy's side and stayed there longest in that dark place, and Oldschool, another hunter, joined them near the end. The way was hard, and Cassidy fell there once and rose again. But the Undead Dynamiters and the Skeletal Miners gave way, four Miners' Union Cards were gathered, and Foreman Thistlenettle was slain and his badge taken.

He climbed out of the mines to Sentinel Tower, where The People's Militia was answered. From there he went on past the edge of Duskwood and through Elwynn Forest into Stormwind City. In the Dwarven District he handed over the badge and the cards, and there he came into new strength and was named a Novice Druid. A picture was taken to mark it. From the Tower of Azora he went looking for the lost guards by Stone Cairn Lake, learned the fate of Rolf, and carried the medallions of Rolf and Malakai to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. He put down Young Forest Bears and Prowlers to protect the frontier. He carried Thomas' report to Goldshire and the armour marker to Sara Timberlain at Eastvale Logging Camp, and in the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch he won six red linen bandanas.

From Menethil Harbor he went out into the Wetlands. At Angerfang Encampment, Slyra, a warrior, stood with him briefly against the Dragonmaw, and Nek'rosh's Gambit was ended. In Mosshide Fen he laboured hard against the gnolls until Tramping Paws was done. He took the gland of a Leech Stalker for Alchemical Hazards, and at last he came to Dun Modr, where the Fall of Dun Modr was told and The Dark Iron War begun.

Next he crossed the sea to Auberdine and Astranaar, carrying Shindrell's Note. He went into Blackfathom Deeps beside Needsmo, a druid of his own calling, and Broven the paladin, and there the Fallenroot and the Tide Priestesses fell. Afterwards Rambleon the hunter joined him, and of all his companions Rambleon stayed with him longest. By a far road through Theramore Isle, the Barrens, Mulgore, Desolace and the Stonetalon Mountains, he came back to Ashenvale. In the Dor'Danil Barrow Den he took the Bottle of Disease from the Forsaken. At Forest Song he delivered the note to Kayneth Stillwind, and in Satyrnaar he freed a Highborne soul for The Tower of Althalaxx.

Last of all, in Menethil Harbor, he found Nord'el, the Bloom of the Heavens, which he must bring to Thundris Windweaver in Auberdine. With Rambleon and Blinkín the mage he walked among The Lost Fleet, ended The Cursed Crew, and took up Lifting the Curse. The bloom was still in his keeping, and the boat at Menethil Harbor was there for the crossing.
