Chapter 16 — The Medallions of the Lost Guards

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up the road again in The Dagger Hills of Westfall. There he struck down the first of the Defias Highwaymen, and he saw the errand called "Thunderbrew" through to its end. Lucia, a paladin, joined him there for a short while. Together they made up the full tally of fifteen highwaymen that "The People's Militia" asked of them. After that he went down into The Deadmines, where the way was hard and he fell more than once in the dark. With Abbigale and Oldschool, both hunters, he pressed on. Undead Dynamiters and Skeletal Miners fell before them, the four Miners' Union Cards were gathered, and Foreman Thistlenettle was overcome and his badge taken. At Sentinel Tower he laid down the Militia's errand, and then he went on through Elwynn Forest to Stormwind City. In the Dwarven District he gave up "Oh Brother..." and "Collecting Memories", and there he came into his twenty-fifth level and was named Novice Druid. He took a picture of that moment.

Elwynn Forest still had work for him. From the Tower of Azora he went out to Stone Cairn Lake and learned what had become of the lost guards and of Rolf. He carried the medallions of Rolf and Malakai to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. He thinned the young forest bears and the prowlers about Ridgepoint Tower, and so "Protect the Frontier" was done. Thomas' report went to Goldshire, the Stormwind Armor Marker went to Sara Timberlain, and six red linen bandanas were gathered at the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch.

From the Lion's Pride Inn his way led to Menethil Harbor and out into the Wetlands. At Angerfang Encampment he fell, and then Slyra, a warrior, stood with him for a little while against the Dragonmaw. With Slyra he finished "Nek'rosh's Gambit" and took up the charge to defeat Nek'rosh. In Mosshide Fen he hunted the mongrels and gnolls until "Tramping Paws" was done, and he cut an unruptured gland from a Leech Stalker for "Alchemical Hazards". He reached Dun Modr and learned of its fall, which led him on to "The Dark Iron War" and to the door of Ironbeard's Tomb. Then he went back to Menethil and took ship across the Great Sea to Darkshore, and from Darkshore went south to Astranaar in Ashenvale. There he was given Shindrell's Note to carry to Kayneth Stillwind. In Blackfathom Deeps he travelled for a time with Needsmo, a druid of his own order, and Broven, a paladin, and together they put down satyrs, tide priestesses and oracles.

Last came a long journey in the company of Rambleon, a hunter. They went by Theramore Isle and Northwatch Hold, across the Southern Barrens and Mulgore, and on through Desolace and the Stonetalon Mountains back into Ashenvale. In The Dor'Danil Barrow Den Cassidy overcame the Forsaken who lurked there and took their Bottle of Disease. At Forest Song he delivered the note and the bottle both, and he was given "Vengeful Trail" and "Insane Druids" to carry forward. In Satyrnaar he freed a Highborne soul for the Tower of Althalaxx, and he went on into Xavian among the Felsworn. At the last he came back through Splintertree Post into Nightsong Woods, and the forest road still ran on beneath the trees.
