Chapter 16 — From the Dagger Hills to the Deeps

Cassidy Darkstar · October 13, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up his road again at Demont's Place and went south into The Dagger Hills, where the Defias Highwaymen kept their watch. There he fell once and rose again, and afterward went on with his errand. He saw the matter of "Thunderbrew" through. Then Lucia, a Paladin, joined him for a short stretch of road. Together they finished the fifteen Highwaymen that "The People's Militia" required, and soon after he passed beneath the earth into The Deadmines.

Below the ground the company changed often. Вольда, a Hunter, walked with him for only a few steps before turning aside. Lucia went her own way there too. Then Abbigale, a Hunter, came and stayed the longest of all who joined him that day. Oldschool, another Hunter, joined them near the end. With them Cassidy overcame the Undead Dynamiters and Undead Excavator and the Skeletal Miners. He gathered the four Miners' Union Cards for "Collecting Memories". He also struck down Foreman Thistlenettle and took his badge for "Oh Brother...". When that work was done his companions left him one by one. He climbed out again and returned to Sentinel Tower, where "The People's Militia" was handed in and ended.

From there he passed the edge of Duskwood and went through Elwynn Forest into Stormwind City. In Old Town he took up "The Color of Blood". In the Dwarven District he gave over the badge and the cards, and there he came into his twenty-fifth level. With it he earned the name of Novice Druid, and a picture was taken to mark it. He also went into the Stormwind Stockade. Then Elwynn called him back, and he did its errands in their proper order. Beyond the Tower of Azora he found the lost guards and learned the fate of Rolf. He carried Rolf's and Malakai's medallions to Guard Thomas at the eastern bridge. He thinned out the young forest bears and the prowlers for "Protect the Frontier". Thomas's report went to Goldshire, the armour marker went to Sara Timberlain, and he gathered red linen bandanas at Brackwell Pumpkin Patch for "Red Linen Goods".

He came next to Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands. There he took up "Fall of Dun Modr" and "Lost in the Thicket Things" and went east through Bluegill Marsh and Sundown Marsh to Angerfang Encampment. In that place Slyra, a Warrior, stood with him for a little while against the Dragonmaw. "Nek'rosh's Gambit" was completed there, and "Defeat Nek'rosh" was taken up in its place. In Mosshide Fen he hunted the gnolls and their mongrels until "Tramping Paws" was fulfilled. From a Leech Stalker he took the gland that "Alchemical Hazards" required. He walked north to Dun Modr and ended that errand, and there "The Dark Iron War" was laid on him. Then he went to Ironbeard's Tomb, and after it back to the harbour.

Last of all he crossed the Great Sea, past the strands of Hillsbrad, to Auberdine in Darkshore. From there he went south into Ashenvale, to Astranaar, where he was given Shindrell's Note to carry to Kayneth Stillwind in Forest Song. He did not go there yet but turned instead to Blackfathom Deeps. Needsmo, a Druid, and Broven, a Paladin, went down with him there for a while, against satyr and priestess and oracle. In the end he came back to the Deepwater Tavern, and the note for Forest Song was still in his keeping.
