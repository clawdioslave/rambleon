Chapter 7 — The Temple Gardens

Cassidy Darkstar · September 29, 2026

Cassidy Darkstar took up his road again in Darnassus, the city of the Night Elves, and on this stage of his journey he went no farther than its paths. No errand was laid upon him, and he sought none. No foe came against him, and he went out against none.

From Darnassus he passed into The Temple Gardens, and that was the whole of his going. He took no quest there and handed none back. He slew no beast, and he gained nothing and lost nothing. He had come into the nineteenth measure of his craft as a druid before he set out, and he left no greater and no less than he came.

Not every stage of a long tale is a march or a battle. A traveller sometimes comes back to a place only to stand in it before the road calls him on again. So it was with Cassidy in The Temple Gardens: he halted there, and there the account of this stage ends.

The road still ran on beyond the gardens, as roads do, and it would be waiting for him wherever he chose to take it up again.
