Chapter 1 — From Thendal to Shen'dar

Axilla Interstellar · September 28, 2026

Axilla Interstellar, Windshaper and Skyborne, began her road in Thendal Village upon Zephras Isle. There she took up the errand called "Coming of Age" and saw it through at once. Then she went into Thendal Grove on the task of "Harmony in Balance", and eight Juvenile Vuldren fell to her bow. In the doing of it she came into her second strength. On her return she learned to Walk on Air, as "Falling With Style" required. She read the Scribbled Note and spoke with Tai'ree Farsight in Thendal Grove, and so the Way of the Hunter was opened to her. She went also to look upon the Anchor Pylon, for the errand of "The Anchors of Zephras" asked it.

Beyond the Grove lay the Thendal Standing Stones, where the Al'Aketh Converts and the Roiling Winds troubled the land. She fell there once. She came back, finished the work the "Agitators" errand had set her, and grew stronger again among the stones. She took many pictures there. Near the Elemental Convergence she used the Gift of Skysight, and then she carried word back as "Return to Rorian" bade her. She gathered fifteen Windstone Clusters. In the Grove she thinned the Pesky Cirrusflies, and afterwards she sought out the Cirrusfly Queen and slew her. She took claws from the Scrawny Ursera. Then she went against the Al'Aketh Thugs, Neophyte and Brute alike, and against their leader Malduko Cloudcrush, and when that work was done none of them stood. In the darkness of Thendal Cave she hunted the Ursera Scavengers and found Urs'anah, the Foul Matriarch, and she bore away her head.

So the first country was put in order, and Axilla took "The Next Step". She passed up through the Shen'dar Highlands to Shen'dar Village. There she sat down by the campfire, as "The Great Outdoors" asked, and took her rest, and in that stillness she reached her sixth strength. Illaya Amberwind welcomed her and told her of the Windshapers, her own people. She spoke with the Innkeeper and learned cooking from Zerril Softbreeze. Then errand after errand was laid upon her: meddlesome mages, criminals, pilfered windstones, prideclaws, hippogryphs, the larders to be restocked, and a warrant for Vulgara the Insatiable.

She went out by Windsong Lake and into the edge of Shadowgale Forest, and then up into the Shen'dar Highlands. There she hunted the Galestriders for their meat, took three Small Eggs for the larders, and gathered ten Prideclaw Pelts. She took more pictures on those slopes than anywhere else. In the midst of that hunting she came into her seventh strength. She drove back the Hippogryph Youth and their Protectors. For the last of that work two companions joined her, Sky, a Druid, and Ferroda, a Hunter like herself. They kept her company for a single hunt, and together they brought down the Hippogryph Matriarch before they went their separate ways. Then Axilla went down again to Shen'dar Village and gave account of the Prideclaws and the hippogryphs. Several errands still waited in the village, and her road went on past it.
