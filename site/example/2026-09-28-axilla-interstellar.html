Chapter 1 — The Matriarchs of Zephras Isle

Axilla Interstellar · September 28, 2026

Axilla Interstellar's tale began in Thendal Village on Zephras Isle, where she took up the errand called Coming of Age and saw it done almost as soon as it was given. Other tasks followed close behind. In Thendal Grove she hunted the Juvenile Vuldren until eight had fallen, as Harmony in Balance required. She used Walk on Air for the errand named Falling With Style, and she went to view the Anchor Pylon, for the Anchors of Zephras were a matter the village wished her to understand. Then she read a Scribbled Note and sought out Tai'ree Farsight in the Grove, and so she set her feet upon the Way of the Hunter, which was her calling.

Beyond the Grove lay the Thendal Standing Stones, and there she went against the Agitators: seven Al'Aketh Converts and six Roiling Winds, overcome one after another. She stopped more than once to take a picture there. She fell once among those stones, but she came back to finish the work. Afterwards she used Skysight near the Elemental Convergence, returned to Rorian, and gathered fifteen Windstone Clusters. The Pesky Cirrusfly troubled the Grove until eight were slain, and then their Queen was slain after them. She took claws from the Scrawny Ursera and broke the Al'Aketh Thugs, Brutes and Neophytes alike, and last of all their captain, Malduko Cloudcrush. Then she went down into Thendal Cave, and there, among the Ursera Scavengers, she slew Urs'anah, the Foul Matriarch, and carried her head back to Thendal Village.

After that came The Next Step, and she left the places of her first learning. She passed through the Shen'dar Highlands and came to Shen'dar Village. There she sat by the campfire, as The Great Outdoors required, and took the good rest that such a fire gives to a traveller. Illaya Amberwind welcomed her and spoke to her of the Windshapers, her own people. She met the Innkeeper, and from Zerril Softbreeze she learned the craft of cooking. More errands were laid upon her in that village than she could yet finish: Meddlesome Mages, Pilfered Windstones, The Criminal Element, A Little Beauty, and the posted warrant for Vulgara the Insatiable. Her wandering took her briefly by Windsong Lake and into the edge of Shadowgale Forest.

In the Shen'dar Highlands she found the Galestriders, and she gathered their meat for the village larders, along with three small eggs. She took ten pelts from the Prideclaws. She drove back the Hippogryph Youths and their Protectors, and there she came into the strength of her seventh level. For a short while two companions joined her on the road, Sky the Druid and Ferroda the Hunter, and with them she brought down the Hippogryph Matriarch before they went their separate ways. Many pictures were taken on those slopes. At last she walked back past the West Pylon Watchtower to Shen'dar Village and gave her reckoning of the Prideclaws and the hippogryphs. Several errands still waited there unfinished, and the road ran on beyond the village.
